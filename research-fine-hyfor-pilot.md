# HYFOR pilot plant under operation

Primetals Technologies commissioned the HYFOR pilot plant at voestalpine's
Donawitz site in April 2021. The first campaigns in April and May processed
about 800 kg of iron ore per test run with 100% hydrogen. The announced
program planned at least two years of multiple campaigns on hematite,
magnetite, and other concentrates.

The pilot has a preheating-oxidation unit, hydrogen reduction unit, dry gas
treatment and dust-recycling system. Concentrate is heated to approximately
900 degrees Celsius; hot DRI exits near 600 degrees Celsius. The supplier
planned hot-briquetting tests and described hot DRI transport to an EAF or
HBI production as downstream options.

The test campaigns provide pilot evidence, not continuous industrial output.
Ore-by-ore metallization, hydrogen consumption, dust recycle, sticking
events, availability, and product handling remain scale-up variables.
