# Circored fine-ore direct reduction

Metso describes Circored as a 100% hydrogen fine-ore direct-reduction process
using a circulating fluidized bed for rapid pre-reduction followed by a
bubbling fluidized bed for final reduction.

The brochure specifies a normal feed range of 0.1-2.0 mm, with up to 6 mm
possible depending on decrepitation. Ultra-fines below 50 micrometres may be
microgranulated with binder to an average size below 0.5 mm; this is a small
agglomeration step even though conventional pellet induration is avoided.

Ore is dried and preheated in a circulating bed up to 900 degrees Celsius.
The first reduction stage reaches up to 80% reduction and the final bubbling
bed above 95%. The supplier states that hydrogen-only reduction below
700 degrees Celsius is used to avoid sticking.

The Trinidad industrial demonstration began operating in 1999 and produced
more than 300,000 tonnes of HBI over several months. The brochure proposes a
nominal commercial line size of 1.25 Mt/y. This historical campaign proves
industrial-scale functionality, but it does not by itself establish a current
long-duration commercial fleet.

For lower-grade ore, Metso proposes a single Circored reduction stage up to
about 85% metallization followed by an electric smelter so that gangue is
removed in slag. Hot direct charging to EAF and HBI for transport are separate
product-handling configurations.
