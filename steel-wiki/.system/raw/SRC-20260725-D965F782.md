# US11959153B2 Production of iron

Rio Tinto-related patent family with priority date 24 October 2016. US11959153B2 was granted 16 April 2024.

The patent describes solid-state direct reduction of iron ore and biomass briquettes under anoxic conditions using electromagnetic energy, principally microwave energy. The ore and biomass absorb microwave energy and generate heat internally; biomass provides the reducing species.

The apparatus includes a preheating furnace, microwave reaction chamber, microwave generator, automatic tuner, waveguide, nitrogen supply, off-gas and particulate outlet, and process control. The described laboratory multimode cavity was 540 x 425 x 425 mm and operated at 2450 MHz with nitrogen at 25 L/min. Trials used about 27 g, approximately four briquettes, at 1 or 2 kW for 1-16 minutes.

The patent discusses 2450 MHz for laboratory testing and 915 MHz for industrial systems above 50 kW. It reports a derived microwave penetration depth of about 5 cm at 915 MHz above 500 C, suggesting volumetric heating of a continuous 5-10 cm thick load.

The reaction sequence detected by XRD was hematite to magnetite to wustite to iron. Fayalite formation from iron oxide and silica was identified as a side reaction. Biomass type and loading changed the proportions of intermediate oxides and metallisation.

The patent reports that a heat-loss-adjusted estimate for small-batch direct reduction was 1.6 GJ/t product, while the unadjusted energy equivalent was around 32 GJ/t briquette feed or 74 GJ/t product. It explicitly notes nearly 90 percent heat loss in the non-optimised feasibility setup and presents about 2 GJ/t as an industrial optimisation possibility, not a demonstrated continuous-plant result.
