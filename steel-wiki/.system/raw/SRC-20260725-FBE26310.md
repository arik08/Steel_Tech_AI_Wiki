# ArcelorMittal Volteron research and scale-up status

ArcelorMittal's R&D overview describes Volteron as a low-temperature iron
electrolysis project that has progressed from technology readiness level 1 to
6. The company states that the electrolyte route uses iron ore, water and
sodium hydroxide at up to approximately 110 degrees Celsius.

The described R&D plant produces one-square-metre iron plates approximately
5 millimetres thick. ArcelorMittal characterises the facility as still being
R&D rather than active industrial commissioning and states that significant
work remains to reach technology readiness level 9.

The article does not confirm that the 40,000 to 80,000 tonne-per-year first
industrial phase announced in 2023 has started construction or production.
