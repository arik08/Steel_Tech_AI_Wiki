# JFE scrap blending optimization using a molten-steel Cu model

- Title: Optimization of Scrap Blending by Estimation Model for Hot Metal Cu
  Concentration in Electric Arc Furnace
- Publisher: JFE Steel Corporation
- Published: date not stated
- URL: https://www.jfe-steel.co.jp/en/products/solution/steelmaking/01-electric-arc-furnace.html
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified technology description

- JFE describes a machine-learning prediction model for the copper
  concentration in molten steel, used together with conventional inventory
  management by scrap brand.
- The stated purpose is to keep tramp-element composition within specification
  while reducing excessive use of expensive high-grade scrap.
- JFE says the solution can be added externally to an existing system.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- JFE's May 2026 solution catalogue separately lists the same technology among
  81 offerings and categorizes it under data utilization and AI.
- The public page gives no deployment site, commissioning date, model error,
  number of heats evaluated, downgrade reduction, raw-material cost saving, or
  independent validation.
- The system predicts and optimizes charge blending. It does not remove copper
  from steel or prove that low-grade scrap can be used for every high-grade
  product.
