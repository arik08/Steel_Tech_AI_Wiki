# Nippon Steel Carbon Neutral Vision 2050

Nippon Steel's March 2021 presentation identifies high-grade production in
large electric arc furnaces as a breakthrough development field. It shows the
Hirohata integrated EAF route for high-grade products and the then-planned
AM/NS Calvert EAF route for third-generation advanced high-strength sheet of
at least 980 MPa and interstitial-free automotive exterior-panel steel.

The presentation states that current high-grade EAF production uses controlled
inputs such as pig iron, internal scrap, and processed scrap. It identifies
scrap-borne impurities including Cu and Sn, nitrogen contamination from air,
large-furnace productivity, DRI melting, and DRI gangue as major challenges.
Its refining diagram distinguishes elements removable to gas or slag from
residual elements such as Cu and Sn that remain in molten steel under ordinary
practice.

The 2021 comparison gives an average EAF size below 100 t per charge, roughly
0.7 Mt/y, versus a Nippon Steel blast furnace example of roughly 4.0 Mt/y. The
figures are a company planning comparison from 2021, not a universal current
capacity limit.
