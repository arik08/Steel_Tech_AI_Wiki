# CORDIS H2PlasmaRed results — public evidence boundary

- Title: Hydrogen Plasma Reduction for Steelmaking and Circular Economy — Results
- Publisher: European Commission CORDIS
- Grant agreement: 101138228
- URL: https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101138228/results
- Last update: 2025-12-18
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified public outputs

- CORDIS reports a project period from 2024-01-01 to 2027-12-31, total cost
  EUR 6,374,937.50 and EU contribution EUR 5,997,965.
- Public deliverables include D1.2, baseline definitions for HPSR in
  steelmaking and sidestream treatment, and D3.2, metallisation and process-time
  optimisation at laboratory scale.
- The results page lists five peer-reviewed publications, including work on
  optical-emission spectra, process parameters and reduction kinetics,
  lab-scale kinetic modelling and in-situ phase transformation.

## Interpretation boundary

- The public result set is meaningful evidence of laboratory research and
  project execution.
- As of the page's 2025-12-18 update, it does not list a public deliverable that
  demonstrates operation of the hundred-kilogram pilot reactor or the
  retrofitted 5 t DC EAF.
- Publication and deliverable counts are not evidence that TRL 7, pilot
  throughput, industrial availability, energy efficiency or product quality
  targets have been achieved.
