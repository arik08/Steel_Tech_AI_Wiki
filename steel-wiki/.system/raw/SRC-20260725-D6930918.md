Source evidence excerpt collected 2026-07-25.

Electra describes its technology as a patented low-temperature electrochemical-
hydrometallurgical system. Iron ore is dissolved in an acidic solution. Iron is
retained while co-minerals are removed and the acid is regenerated. Electricity
then electrodeposits iron onto metal sheets. The resulting iron can be used in
electric arc furnaces or iron-based batteries.

The company states that the process can synchronize with intermittent energy,
can use previously mined and unused iron-bearing materials, produces 99% pure
iron, removes co-minerals such as silica and alumina, and uses repeatable modular
cell units.
