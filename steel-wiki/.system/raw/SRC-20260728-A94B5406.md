# WA Government Priority Projects — Project NeoSmelt

- Official page: https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/department-of-the-premier-and-cabinet/priority-projects
- Last updated: 2026-07-23
- Project NeoSmelt is one of Western Australia's first eight designated Priority Projects.
- Priority Project designation provides fast-tracked approvals, reduced process duplication, strategic recognition, and coordinated government engagement and support while maintaining regulatory standards.
- The Office of the Coordinator General coordinates and facilitates government approvals and assessment processes.
- Projects are typically supported until final investment decision or until they no longer require OCG support.
- Evidence boundary: Priority Project designation is an approvals-coordination status, not an approval, final investment decision, construction approval, construction milestone, commissioning milestone or operating result.
