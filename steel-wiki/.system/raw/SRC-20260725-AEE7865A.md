# Rio Tinto Investor Seminar 2023 BioIron presentation

At Rio Tinto's 6 December 2023 investor seminar, the company described blending Pilbara ores with agricultural biomass waste and processing the pellets through a microwave furnace to produce an iron/HBI-type pellet.

The company described a downstream melter separating ore oxides from iron, followed by basic oxygen steelmaking. BioIron therefore produces a solid metallised intermediate rather than finished steel and requires downstream impurity removal and steelmaking.

Rio Tinto claimed up to 95 percent lower CO2 emissions versus BF-BOF when using renewable microwave electricity and biomass carbon circulation. It also claimed about 65 percent lower electricity demand than a renewable-hydrogen shaft-furnace route. These are company comparative claims and require consistent system boundaries, biomass life-cycle accounting, ore grade, downstream melting, and electricity assumptions.
