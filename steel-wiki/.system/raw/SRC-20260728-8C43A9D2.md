# Nucor Q1 2026 West Virginia sheet mill update

- Document: First Quarter 2026 earnings call presentation
- Publisher: Nucor Corporation
- Project status: Nucor described the project as on time and on budget, with commissioning sequenced throughout 2026.
- At the reporting point, pickle-line commissioning was underway; the cold mill, construction galvanizing line, automotive galvanizing line, melt shop, and hot mill were scheduled to enter commissioning in sequence through year-end.
- Production was expected in 2027, with a deliberate multi-phase ramp and expanding capacity utilisation and product capabilities through 2027–2028.
- Boundary: commissioning of individual lines does not mean the integrated mill has started commercial steel shipments.
