# K1-MET SuS-F SuSteel follow-up

K1-MET describes SuS-F as the follow-up project for hydrogen plasma smelting
reduction at the voestalpine Stahl Donawitz pilot plant.

Its stated objectives are semi-continuous HPSR with continuous fine-ore feed
and semi-continuous low-carbon-steel tapping, integrated green-hydrogen
supply, off-gas utilization, recovery of water vapor for possible
electrolysis, modern plant automation and digitalization, and development
toward TRL 7. The TRL 7 item is a project objective, not evidence that TRL 7
has already been reached.

K1-MET lists the project period as 2022-10-01 to 2025-09-30 and identifies
voestalpine Stahl, K1-MET, Montanuniversitaet Leoben, Pirhofer Automation,
voestalpine Stahl Donawitz, and WIVA P&G as consortium members.
