# Adami et al. — HPSR pilot-plant experimental results

The peer-reviewed open-access article describes the K1-MET pilot HPSR plant at
voestalpine Stahl Donawitz. The reactor is a gas-tight DC arc furnace using a
hollow graphite cathode to feed process gas and material near the plasma. The
reported setup has a capacity of roughly 100 kg iron ore per trial and was
commissioned in 2021.

The paper reports that internal thermal insulation improved slag-metal phase
separation and lowered energy consumption within the compared trials, without
claiming a commercial plant energy intensity. A phosphate-bonded magnesia
working lining showed less wear than the compared magnesia-alumina spinel
lining. Gangue composition materially affected reduction behaviour.

The five reported trials were batch-charged and intentionally stopped in a
semi-reduced state after about 90 minutes to compare kinetics. The reactor did
not have melt tapping for those trials; after cooling, the slag and metal
phases were mechanically removed. These limitations are essential when
interpreting the pilot results.

The article reports TRL 5 and a roadmap toward TRL 6–7 by the end of 2026; the
latter is a target rather than a demonstrated status.
