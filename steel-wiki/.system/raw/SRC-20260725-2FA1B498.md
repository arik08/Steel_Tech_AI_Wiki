# K1-MET SuSteel project

K1-MET describes SuSteel as an R&D project for carbon-dioxide-free crude-steel
production using hydrogen plasma. Hydrogen reduces iron oxides, while the
plasma supplies thermal energy for melting; water vapor is the intended
reaction by-product.

The research focuses included plasma generation and maintenance, hydrogen
effects on refractory lining, process conditions, and operating parameters.
A laboratory reactor at Montanuniversitaet Leoben had about 100 g melt
capacity. Results were transferred to a pilot reactor with about 90 kg melt
capacity for batch-mode operating and control studies.

The original project ran from 2016-09-01 to 2020-02-29, with follow-up work
until June 2023. The consortium comprised K1-MET, Montanuniversitaet Leoben,
and voestalpine Stahl. These capacities are melt or batch capability, not
continuous hourly production.
