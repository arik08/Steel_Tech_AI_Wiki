# Conceptualizing Hydrogen Plasma Reduction for Industrial-Scale Prereduced Iron Ore Smelting: A Scenario-Based Approach

Verified source: AISTech 2025 Proceedings of the Iron & Steel Technology
Conference, 5–8 May 2025, Nashville, Tennessee, USA.

Official abstract:
https://imis.aist.org/AISTPapers/Abstracts_Only_PDF/PR-389-235.pdf
DOI: https://doi.org/10.33313/389/243

Authors: T. K. Wickham, E. K. L. Kleynhans, S. C. Louw,
I. J. Geldenhuys.

The paper uses process- and scenario-based requirements engineering to
conceptualize industrial-scale HPSR of pre-reduced ore and a preliminary
techno-economic assessment. The authors explicitly describe a public evidence
gap between current HPSR development and industrial-scale commercial
feasibility.

The abstract reports the authors' assessment of HPSR at TRL 5 and argues that
industrial-scale end requirements should be fed back into smaller-scale test
programs.

Evidence boundary: TRL 5 is the conference-paper authors' assessment, not an
official project certification. The public source is an abstract page, so
unpublished detailed techno-economic values are not inferred.
