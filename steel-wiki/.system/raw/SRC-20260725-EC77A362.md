# JFE Steel carbon-neutral technology priorities

JFE Steel said it would step up development and begin sequential trials of carbon-
recycling blast furnaces, hydrogen direct-reduction steelmaking and large-scale EAFs.
It planned conversion to a large EAF at Kurashiki and construction of a stainless-steel
EAF at Chiba. This management statement establishes the technology portfolio and planned
trial direction; it does not by itself demonstrate commercial hydrogen-DRI operation.
