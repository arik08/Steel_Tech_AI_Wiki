# Sweden government Boden Industrial Park rail notes

- Page: Infrastruktur- och bostadsminister Andreas Carlson inviger järnvägen till Boden Industrial Park
- Publisher: Government Offices of Sweden, Ministry of Rural Affairs and Infrastructure
- Published: 2026-06-08
- URL: https://regeringen.se/pressmeddelanden/2026/06/infrastruktur--och-bostadsminister-andreas-carlson-inviger-jarnvagen-till-boden-industrial-park/
- Evidence type: Swedish government press release.
- Confirmed milestone:
  - on 9 June 2026 the minister formally inaugurated the new railway connecting Boden Industrial Park with the Iron Ore Line and the national rail network.
- Verification boundary:
  - the release confirms shared industrial-park logistics infrastructure, not completion or commissioning of Stegra’s steelmaking process units;
  - the rail project is described as public-private cooperation and should not be represented as an asset built solely by Stegra.
