# JFE Chiba No. 4 steelmaking shop EAF start-up

- Title: JFE Steel East Japan Works (Chiba) No. 4 Steelmaking Shop EAF
  start-up and completion ceremony
- Publisher: JFE Steel Corporation
- Published: 2026-04-30
- URL: https://www.jfe-steel.co.jp/release/2026/04/260430.html
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified status and equipment

- JFE held the completion ceremony on **2026-04-28** and began operating the
  electric arc furnace on **2026-04-29**.
- The No. 4 steelmaking shop produces stainless steel. The new EAF is used to
  replace part of the blast-furnace hot-metal input with scrap rather than
  representing a complete site conversion away from hot metal.
- JFE reports approximately **JPY 18 billion** of investment, about
  **300,000 tonnes/year** of scrap-melting capacity and an expected maximum
  GHG reduction of approximately **450,000 tonnes/year**.
- JFE states that scrap-melting capability is up to about six times the prior
  level.

## Interpretation boundary

- The operating date is an achieved milestone. Capacity and GHG reduction are
  design or expected values, not measured annual results.
- This is a stainless-steel shop transition project and is distinct from JFE's
  planned approximately 2 Mt/y innovative large EAF at Kurashiki.
- The route still uses blast-furnace hot metal; it must not be described as a
  fully scrap-only or DRI-only EAF route.
