Electra announced commissioning of its pilot plant in Boulder, Colorado on
2024-03-27. The company says the pilot produces metallic iron from already
mined, high-impurity, commercially stranded ores. It states that the process
operates at 140 degrees Fahrenheit, can integrate intermittent renewable energy,
and produces clean iron with greater than 99 percent purity. The pilot is
designed to produce approximately one-square-metre iron plates and increase
capacity in phases to validate modularity.
