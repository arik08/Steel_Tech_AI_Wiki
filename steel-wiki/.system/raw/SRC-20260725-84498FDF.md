# Rio Tinto climate reporting: 2025 progress and 2026 action

Rio Tinto's current climate reporting records construction of the BioIron pilot plant as paused in 2025 due to technical and design challenges.

For 2026, Rio Tinto states that it will continue BioIron technology development to minimise technical risks and optimise performance.

This distinguishes the paused construction project from continued technology R&D and supersedes the earlier 2026 commissioning target as the current project status.
