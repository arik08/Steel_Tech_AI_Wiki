# Metso — Green steel smelting successfully piloted at Pori

- Original: https://www.metso.com/campaigns/smelting-newsletter/smelting-insights/green-steel-smelting-production-successfully-piloted-in-metso-research-center/
- Publisher: Metso
- Published: 2025-12-11
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified campaign results

- Metso describes a 3-in-line Pori DRI smelting pilot able to process feed at up to 1,000 kg/h.
- The pilot was hot commissioned and two test campaigns were completed in 2025.
- The campaigns targeted equipment hot commissioning and the carburization behavior of high- and low-carbon feed materials.
- Metso reports that both campaigns achieved the target hot-metal carbon level, but the public article does not disclose the numerical target or achieved carbon values.
- The campaigns also investigated element distribution, slag properties, refractory performance and equipment behavior.
- Metso says laboratory-scale comparison supported scale-up readiness and that preparations for the first customer pilot were ongoing.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- Metso's 2025 Sustainability statement independently confirms successful hot commissioning in a Pori DRI Smelting Furnace test campaign.
- Metso's Smelting Issue 1/2025 front page separately states that two test campaigns had been conducted.
- All three confirmations are from the same corporate source family. They verify what Metso publicly reported, but do not constitute independent customer or academic validation.
- The two completed campaigns should not be described as customer campaigns: the detailed article explicitly says preparation for the first customer pilot was still ongoing.
- No numerical campaign results for carbon, energy, metallization, availability, refractory wear or hot-metal composition are published in the article.
