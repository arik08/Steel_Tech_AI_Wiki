# Tata Steel first-in-world digital twin in sinter making

Tata Steel announced on 2022-01-11 that its sinter-making digital twin used a
cognitive soft sensor to recommend changes to control levers for process
stability and KPI performance.

The company reported that the innovation reduced return fines and cut stack
emissions by 60%, producing savings. The announcement presented the system as a
first-in-world sinter-process replica and one of the cases recognised in the CII
Industrial Innovation Awards 2021.

The release confirms a deployed use case and quantitative company-reported
outcome. It does not disclose the baseline period, absolute emissions,
confidence interval, number of sinter lines, persistence of the result or
whether control actions were automatically closed-loop or operator-approved.
