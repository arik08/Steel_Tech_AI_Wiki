# Nippon Steel carbon-neutral and digital R&D

Nippon Steel identifies three main process technologies: hydrogen direct reduction,
high-grade production in large EAFs, and hydrogen injection into blast furnaces.
Demonstration work uses a test blast furnace at Kimitsu and test electric and shaft
furnaces at the Hasaki R&D Center. The company reported a 43% CO2 reduction in its
2024 test-furnace campaign. It also conducts CCUS R&D, including blast-furnace-gas CO2
separation and capture. Digital R&D covers remote operation, automation, AI and advanced
algorithms; the page describes an R&D program rather than fleet-wide deployment.
