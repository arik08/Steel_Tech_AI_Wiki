# Nippon Steel confirms Big River DRI investment

- Title: U. S. Steel Announces Investment to Build a Direct Reduced Iron Plant
  at its Electric Arc Furnace Mini-Mill
- Publisher: Nippon Steel Corporation
- Published: 2026-04-30
- URL: https://www.nipponsteel.com/en/newsroom/news/2026/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2026/05/01/20260430_150.pdf
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified investment decision

- U. S. Steel's board approved construction of a DRI plant at Big River Steel
  Works in Osceola, Arkansas on **2026-04-28**.
- Nippon Steel reports an investment of approximately **USD 1.9 billion** as
  part of its wider U.S. investment commitment.
- The project links U. S. Steel's Keetac DR-grade pellet supply in Minnesota to
  DRI production and EAF steelmaking at Big River.

## Interpretation boundary

- This announcement confirms the investment decision and location, not
  construction completion or production.
- The one-page Nippon Steel release does not disclose plant capacity,
  technology supplier, reductant mix or start-up date; those details require
  separate supplier and industry sources.
