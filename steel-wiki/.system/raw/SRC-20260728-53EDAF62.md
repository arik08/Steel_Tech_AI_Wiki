# ArcelorMittal 2025 Form 20-F: Volteron progress

- Reporting period: year ended 2025-12-31
- Filing/publication: 2026
- ArcelorMittal reports that the Volteron 1:1 vertical-cell pilot operated successfully.
- It also reports that John Cockerill completed the engineering design for a full-scale industrial plant.
- ArcelorMittal characterises the combined work as demonstrating techno-economic feasibility.
- Boundary: the filing does not state that the planned 40,000–80,000 t/y step-up plant has reached FID, construction, commissioning, or commercial operation.
- Cross-check: the European Commission INCITE entry records operation of the 1 m² pilot from April 2025 but treats the larger plant as conditional on pilot results, regulation, business conditions, and financing.
