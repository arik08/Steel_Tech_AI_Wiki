# JFE AI scrap automatic inspection enters operation

- Title: JFE launches proprietary automatic scrap inspection technology as a
  JFE Resolus solution
- Publisher: JFE Steel Corporation
- Published: 2026-06-29
- URL: https://www.jfe-steel.co.jp/release/2026/06/260629.html
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified status and function

- JFE installed its AI-based automatic scrap inspection technology at the
  Sendai Works in **April 2026**, validated and tuned it on actual equipment,
  and began operation in **June 2026**.
- The system automatically photographs a truck bed while scrap is unloaded and
  uses the images to judge scrap-grade composition and foreign-material
  contamination.
- JFE states that the achieved accuracy is suitable for on-site appraisal work.
  The release does not disclose a numerical accuracy, confusion matrix,
  throughput or false-accept rate.
- The system is intended to reduce inspection labor, standardize appraisal and
  digitize inspection data for later operational and quality optimization.
- JFE added the technology to its JFE Resolus product portfolio for internal
  rollout and external licensing.

## Interpretation boundary

- This is a receiving-inspection and classification system. It is not evidence
  that tramp elements such as copper are chemically removed from scrap.
- "Suitable accuracy" is a company statement without public quantitative test
  data in the release.
