# ZESTY pilot flash-reactor paper

Mokhtarani et al., “New Insights Into Hydrogen Reduction of Hematite in an
Indirectly Heated Flash Reactor from Measurements and First-Order Modeling,”
Metallurgical and Materials Transactions B 56 (2025) 1928–1941, was published
10 February 2025 (DOI 10.1007/s11663-025-03465-3).

The paper reports a vertical, indirectly heated tubular ZESTY pilot reactor.
Fine ore, generally below 500 micrometres, is gravity-fed from the top while
hydrogen enters from the base in counter-current flow. The measured reactor
tube had 0.2 m inner diameter, 18 m length, and 18 independently controlled
electric heaters. Tests used uniform wall temperatures from 850 to 1050 °C,
60 kg/h semi-continuous ore feed and a hydrogen stoichiometric ratio of two.
The article reports metallisation up to 95 percent across the investigated
conditions; this is not a continuous 30,000 t/y demonstration-plant result.

The authors describe the proposed scale-up method as parallel modules of
full-scale reactor tubes with material preheating and heat recovery. They also
identify particle size, hydrogen stoichiometry, temperature, residence time,
heat/mass transfer and possible high-temperature sticking as material
performance variables or remaining risks.
