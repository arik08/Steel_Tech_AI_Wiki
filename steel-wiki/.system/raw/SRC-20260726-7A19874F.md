# Rio Tinto Steel Decarbonisation and Biomass Ironmaking

Verified source: official Association for Iron & Steel Technology conference
material hosted by AIST.

Presentation:
https://www.aist.org/AIST/aist/AIST/Conferences_Exhibitions/Training_Seminars/Scrap%20Files/18-Leigh-and-Buckley.pdf

Presenter: Michael Buckley, Manager Steel Decarbonisation, Rio Tinto.
Presentation date: 2023-03-08.

The presentation describes the BioIron development path from initial biomass
tests through a small-scale pilot and the planned 1 t/h continuous pilot. The
flowsheet blends fine iron ore, flux, and raw biomass into green briquettes,
preheats and pre-reduces them above 600 °C, applies microwave energy for
metallisation, and sends the carbon-containing DRI to an electric melter for
gangue removal and pig-iron production.

It also reports testing of agricultural, aquatic, and forestry residues and
states that biomass is used without pre-charring.

Evidence boundary: this is a company conference presentation. Development
history and design statements are Rio Tinto claims; the 1 t/h item was a plan,
not evidence of completed continuous operation.
