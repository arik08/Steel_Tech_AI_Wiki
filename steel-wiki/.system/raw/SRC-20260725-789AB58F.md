# Tata Steel Nederland proposed DRP-EAF-CCS route

Under a non-binding joint letter of intent, Tata Steel Nederland aims to replace Blast
Furnace 7 and Coke and Gas Plant 2 with a direct-reduction plant and EAF. The DRP would
initially use natural gas. CCS would subsequently be added, while biomethane and/or
hydrogen would be phased in when economical at scale. Engineering, schedule, total
spend and final investment decision were not complete. Dutch support of up to €2 billion
was contemplated, subject to conditions.
