# Boston Metal Woburn WARN and financing setback - Boston.com notes

- Publisher: Boston.com
- Author: Beth Treffeisen
- Published: 2026-02-24
- URL: https://www.boston.com/news/business/2026/02/24/layoffs-hit-boston-metal-as-company-plans-woburn-shutdown/
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified report

Boston.com reported that Boston Metal notified Massachusetts of a planned
71-person workforce reduction and closure of its three Woburn locations: the
pilot/R&D facility, main office and warehouse. The original effective date was
March 14, 2026.

The company notice linked the action to a January 30 industrial accident at its
Brazil critical-metals facility. The resulting equipment failure halted those
industrial operations and prevented a financing milestone, which removed
capital needed to continue Woburn operations.

Boston Metal also told the Boston Business Journal that the cost and complexity
of MOE steel development had exceeded current revenue and available capital,
and that it was restructuring around the metals business and a lower burn rate.

This report establishes the financing and workforce risk at the February
notice date. It does not prove that all Woburn facilities ultimately closed,
because later funding changed the layoff schedule.
