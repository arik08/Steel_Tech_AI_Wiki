# Nucor Convent DRI carbon capture agreement

ExxonMobil agreed to capture, transport and store up to 800,000 tonnes of CO2 per year
from Nucor's direct-reduced-iron site in Convent, Louisiana. Start-up was expected in
2026 when announced. The source concerns CCS attached to a DRI facility, not a blast
furnace, and does not identify the DRI reductant as hydrogen. Nucor's 2025 Form 10-K
continued to disclose the agreement, but this capture does not establish commissioning.
