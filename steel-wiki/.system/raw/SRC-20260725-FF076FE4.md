# POSCO decarbonization technology status

POSCO Group described its phased steel decarbonization strategy on 2025-10-29.
Short- and medium-term bridge technologies include blast-furnace efficiency,
hydrogen-rich gas injection, low-carbon fuels and raw materials, electric arc
furnaces, low hot-metal-ratio operation, and CCUS. HyREX hydrogen reduction
ironmaking is the long-term route.

POSCO reported that a natural-gas injection system was installed for blast
furnaces in the first half of 2023 and that hydrogen injection technology is
under development through the COOLSTAR national R&D project.

For CCUS, POSCO reported multiple demonstration paths. A January 2024 Pohang
Works test confirmed injection of medium-purity captured CO2 into coke ovens
and an increase in coke-oven-gas calorific value. POSCO Holdings is also
developing mineral carbonation and POSCO is evaluating phased capture and
storage for coke ovens, sinter plants, hot-blast stoves and power plants. These
activities do not constitute completed fleet-wide blast-furnace CCS.

POSCO described HyREX as a fluidized-bed-reduction route based on FINEX that
can directly use iron-ore fines. It said the main facilities of a 300,000
tonne-per-year demonstration plant at Pohang were being jointly designed and
that commercialization technology is targeted for completion through
demonstration by 2030. These are company plans and assessments.

The article also states that data- and AI-powered intelligent factories are
used to improve energy efficiency and workplace safety.
