# SSAB HYBRIT pilot results

SSAB reported that the HYBRIT partnership had produced more than 5,000 tonnes of
hydrogen-reduced iron at the Luleå pilot and demonstrated the mine-to-steel chain at
semi-industrial scale. Research covered fossil-free hydrogen direct reduction and
melting sponge iron in an EAF. The pilot report cited less than 0.05 tCO2e per tonne
of steel for the tested route, excluding a claim of zero absolute lifecycle emissions.
The program moved to industrialisation support after the pilot phase.
