# Stegra closes EUR 1.4 billion financing round

Stegra announced on 2026-06-24 that it had closed a EUR 1.4 billion financing
round led by a Wallenberg Investments consortium. The company said the round
was intended to complete construction and commissioning of its integrated
green-steel plant in Boden, Sweden.

The round followed several slower months during financing activities. Stegra
continued to state that the project timeline was under review. Therefore the
funding close is evidence that a major financing condition was satisfied, but
it is not evidence that the previously publicised startup or first-delivery
dates remain unchanged.
