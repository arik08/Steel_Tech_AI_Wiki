# Rio Tinto pauses BioIron pilot construction, 2025

Rio Tinto announced on 17 November 2025 that the Kwinana site previously earmarked for the BioIron R&D facility and associated pilot plant would instead be available for a proposed Calix Zesty demonstration plant.

Rio Tinto determined that the current BioIron furnace design required additional development to minimise technical risks and optimise performance. Construction of the BioIron pilot was paused.

Rio Tinto stated that it remained committed to the long-term potential of BioIron and that research and development would continue with the University of Nottingham and Metso.

The release reiterates that BioIron uses agricultural by-products such as wheat straw, barley straw, sugarcane bagasse, rice stalks, and canola straw as reductant. It states that BioIron electricity consumption is about one-third of that required by steelmaking processes relying on renewable hydrogen. This is a company claim and is not a disclosed plant operating result.
