# Swedish Energy Agency review of HYBRIT pilot phase

The Swedish Energy Agency's 2024 analysis of industrial transition states that
HYBRIT's principal pilot project ran from 2018 until summer 2024.

The government analysis records successful long-term operation of alkaline
electrolysers for hydrogen production and storage and development of an
effective operating practice for melting fossil-free sponge iron into crude
steel in an electric arc furnace. It describes the pilot as completed and the
technology as moving toward larger-scale production.

The analysis is an independent government summary. It does not provide a full
campaign dataset, plant availability, product-by-product quality statistics or
audited production economics.
