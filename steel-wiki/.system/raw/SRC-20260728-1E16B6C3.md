# Nucor current sheet-products page — West Virginia product scope

- Title: Steel Sheet Products: American-Made EAF Steel
- Publisher: Nucor Corporation
- Published date: not stated
- URL: https://nucor.com/products/sheet/
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified current product scope

- In its current first-party sheet-products page, Nucor says the West Virginia
  sheet mill will be able to produce 84-inch sheet products.
- The page lists a 76-inch tandem cold mill and two galvanizing lines capable
  of producing advanced high-end automotive and construction grades.
- The wording is prospective ("when operational"), so it confirms the planned
  product and equipment scope rather than completed production capability.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- The current product page independently restates the scope in Nucor's
  2022 project-selection release.
- The current page does not restate the earlier detail that the automotive
  galvanizing line will have "full inspection" capability. The product-scope
  claim should therefore treat that phrase as an announced design detail from
  the 2022 release, while the broader 84-inch, 76-inch and two-line scope is
  currently reconfirmed.
- The page does not establish integrated mill start-up, shipments, product
  qualification or achieved line performance.
