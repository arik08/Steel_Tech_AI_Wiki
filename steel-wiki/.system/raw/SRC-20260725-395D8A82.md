# ArcelorMittal Germany DRI-EAF decision

ArcelorMittal stated that it could not proceed with the previously announced DRI and
EAF investments at Bremen and Eisenhüttenstadt. It cited weak project economics,
high electricity costs, slow progress toward viable green hydrogen, and the lack of
competitiveness of natural-gas DRI as an interim route. The company described a phased
European approach that starts with EAF investments where the business case is stronger.
This is a company statement about project status, not proof that hydrogen DRI has been
abandoned across the entire group.
