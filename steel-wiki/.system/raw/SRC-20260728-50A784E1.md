# CORDIS D-CRBN project closure record

- Source: European Commission CORDIS, D-CRBN grant agreement 101161563
- URL: https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101161563
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Page last update shown by CORDIS: 2025-10-20

## Confirmed points

- CORDIS currently labels the EIC-funded D-CRBN project “Project closed”.
- The recorded project period is 1 July 2024 through 30 June 2026.
- Total cost is €3,607,500 and EU contribution is €2,499,999.
- The project objective is plasma-based conversion of captured CO2 into value-added products for hard-to-abate industries.

## Interpretation boundary

- The “Project closed” label is an administrative funding-record status after the scheduled end date. It does not establish that the ArcelorMittal Gent integrated trial achieved a specified conversion rate, CO output, purity, electricity intensity, continuous run duration, or commercial deployment decision.
- The CORDIS page does not publish project-result deliverables or quantified Gent trial outcomes.
- The coordinator’s efficiency language in the project objective is not treated as independently validated performance.
