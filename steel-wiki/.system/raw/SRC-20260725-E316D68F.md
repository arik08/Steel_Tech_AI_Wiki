# Construction starts for HYFOR and Smelter demonstration plant

Primetals Technologies announced on 2025-09-25 that construction had started
on the HY4SMELT demonstration plant in Linz, Austria, with voestalpine, Rio
Tinto and other partners.

The plant combines HYFOR hydrogen-based fine-ore reduction with an electric
smelter. Primetals states that HYFOR processes iron-ore fines without an
agglomeration step and that the project will focus on low-to-medium-grade ores.
The demonstration plant is designed for a capacity of 3 tonnes per hour and
startup is scheduled for the end of 2027.

The schedule and performance descriptions are supplier and project-partner
targets. Construction is not evidence that continuous demonstration or
commercial operation has been completed.
