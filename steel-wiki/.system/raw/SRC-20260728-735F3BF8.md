# Tata Steel NSE clarification on Jharkhand capital projects

- Title: News Clarification (Ref. SEC/2018/2025-26)
- Publisher: Tata Steel Limited / National Stock Exchange of India
- Published: 2026-03-05
- URL: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/NIDHIFADNAVIS_05032026095816_NSE-Clarification_signed.pdf
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified clarification

- Responding to a report that Tata Steel would invest ₹11,000 crore in
  Jharkhand for advanced-grade steel, Tata Steel said its board had approved
  or was deliberating several capital projects across operating units,
  including Jharkhand.
- The filing identifies the previously approved Tinplate expansion and Combi
  Mill and says the company continues to evaluate additional projects involving
  HIsarna and EASyMelt.
- It characterizes the activities as part of the company's overall capital
  allocation framework rather than confirming a single new ₹11,000 crore
  HIsarna investment.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- The Jharkhand government announcement (SRC-20260728-433E5CF1) describes a
  proposed package above ₹11,000 crore and an estimated ₹7,000 crore component
  for HIsarna and EASyMelt.
- Tata Steel's FY2025-26 Integrated Report (SRC-20260728-1BFBFBDF) prints
  approximately ₹11.1 billion for the LoI/MoU, a tenfold difference from the
  government package figure.
- The exchange clarification supports treating these figures as a multi-project
  package and evaluation framework, not HIsarna-only committed CAPEX, FID,
  awarded EPC, construction expenditure, or a reconciled final budget.
