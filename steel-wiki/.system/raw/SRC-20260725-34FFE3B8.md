# US DOE projects for steel scrap purification and copper removal

The U.S. Department of Energy selected a Sortera Technologies-led project for
USD 3 million of federal funding to demonstrate a modular system for removing
copper contaminants from shredded steel scrap. The proposed process combines
AI-assisted image recognition sorting with a thermo-mechanical separation
step.

DOE also selected an MIT-led molten sulfide electrolysis project with Rio
Tinto for USD 5.6 million of federal funding. DOE states that the proposed
process could use lower-grade ores and high-copper scrap and could be
integrated with steelmaking to remove copper impurities.

These amounts are federal funding selections for development and
demonstration, not proof that either process has reached commercial-scale
operation.
