# Metso opens a DRI smelting furnace pilot facility in Pori

Metso announced on 25 October 2024 that it had opened a DRI Smelting Furnace
pilot facility at its research centre in Pori, Finland. The facility is intended
for customer-specific feed testing and industrial scale-up work.

Metso describes the furnace as a three-in-line pilot with capacity up to one
tonne of hot metal per hour and an investment of EUR 8 million. The supplier
stated that hot commissioning and customer campaigns would follow the opening.
The opening is evidence of a pilot test platform, not of a commercial steel
plant.

The supplier positions the furnace between a direct reduction plant and
existing downstream steelmaking, with the ability to handle high slag levels
and a wider raw-material range than scrap-oriented furnaces.
