# Metso DRI Smelting Furnace technical details

Metso's product page, updated in October 2025, describes the Pori pilot DRI
Smelting Furnace as a rectangular three-electrodes-in-line unit with a
2.5 MVA transformer and approximately one tonne per hour hot-metal capacity.
It has four material-feeding arrangements, 200 mm graphite electrodes,
separate hot-metal and slag tapping, and a 1.8 m by 2.8 m hearth. Metso states
that a typical one-week test campaign requires about 50 tonnes of feed.

The supplier positions the pilot as a customer feed-testing and scale-up
platform because DRI iron content, impurities, metallisation, and carbon vary
by ore body and reduction process.

Metso also publishes a conceptual commercial design exceeding 1.2 Mt/y hot
metal, with a six-in-line electrode layout, up to 115 MVA apparent power and
110 MW design power. Claims of less than 3% slag FeO and greater than 95%
availability are supplier design claims, not operating results from the Pori
pilot or an identified commercial low-emission installation.
