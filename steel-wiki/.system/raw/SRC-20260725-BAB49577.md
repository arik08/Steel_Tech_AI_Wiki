# Primetals Technologies and POSCO HyREX demonstration cooperation

Primetals Technologies and POSCO signed a cooperation agreement on
2024-07-22 to design and implement a HyREX demonstration plant at POSCO's
Pohang Works. The supplier announced the agreement on 2024-09-19, following
an earlier memorandum of understanding in 2022.

The announced equipment train includes an ore dryer, cascaded fluidized-bed
reactors, hot-DRI transport, an electric smelting furnace, dedusting, metal
tapping, and slag granulation. It is intended to use sinter-feed fine ore
directly and melt DRI fines in the ESF.

The objective is to test process details and identify cost-effective operating
parameters. This is a demonstration-plant design and implementation program,
not evidence of completed continuous operation.
