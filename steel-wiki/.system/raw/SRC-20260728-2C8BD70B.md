# ArcelorMittal Belgium 2026 — Steelanol bioreactor 2 repair and leak test

- Title: Geluidshinder door testen bij Steelanol
- Publisher: ArcelorMittal Belgium
- Published: 2026-05-07
- URL: https://belgium.arcelormittal.com/nieuws-publicaties/nieuws/geluidshinder-Steelanol
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified current-status signal

- ArcelorMittal Belgium announced that, after repairs to Steelanol bioreactor 2,
  it would perform a nitrogen pressure leak test on 2026-05-08.
- The notice describes the leak test as a standard safety step before returning
  the installation to service.
- Temporary venting noise was expected when the reactor was depressurised.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- This official neighbourhood notice is a strong dated signal that Steelanol
  equipment was still being repaired and tested in May 2026. It contradicts any
  interpretation that the June 2025 closure warning proved an earlier permanent
  shutdown.
- A leak test before return to service does not prove that bioreactor 2
  subsequently restarted, that all four reactors were available, or that the
  plant reached nameplate output.
- No audited production, availability, yield or closure decision is disclosed.
