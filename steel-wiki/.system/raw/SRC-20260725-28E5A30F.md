# POSCO AI blast furnace and smart steelworks baseline

POSCO reported on 2020-01-20 that Pohang Works had been selected as a World
Economic Forum Lighthouse Factory in July 2019. The company described Pohang
No. 2 blast furnace as an AI-enabled furnace combining worker expertise with
standardized operating data, IoT sensors, cameras, and deep learning.

POSCO stated that the system could predict and control operating variables and
that the smart-furnace implementation increased daily molten-iron production
by 240 tonnes. POSCO also described PosFrame as its platform for continuous
steelmaking processes, connecting data across the integrated works for
traceability and diagnosis.

This source establishes an operating smart-steelworks baseline as of 2020; it
does not by itself verify the current deployment scope across every POSCO
facility.
