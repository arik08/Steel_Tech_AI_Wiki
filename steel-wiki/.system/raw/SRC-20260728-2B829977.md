## Verified scope

The official AISTech gallery confirms that H2PlasmaRed progress was presented
in the Oxygen Steelmaking track. The abstract describes a project objective of
advancing HPSR from TRL 5 to TRL 7 using a pilot-scale reactor and a retrofitted
5-ton arc furnace to investigate low-grade ores and sidestream materials.

The public gallery does not provide the full paper, operating campaign data,
throughput, availability, energy consumption, metal yield or proof that TRL 7
has already been achieved. Therefore it supports the presentation event and
declared demonstration scope only.
