# Liu et al. 2026 HYBRIT LRC thermodynamics preprint notes

- Title: Operational thermodynamics and hydrogen embrittlement risk in lined rock cavern–shaft systems: Numerical insights from Sweden’s first LRC hydrogen storage pilot
- DOI: 10.2139/ssrn.6882937
- Posted: January 2026
- Confirmed authors visible in the discovery record: Xin Liu; Zongze Li; Xilin Shi; Xinxing Wei (record indicates six authors in total).
- Evidence type: SSRN preprint; not peer reviewed.
- Scope: coupled non-isothermal real-gas flow and heat-transfer framework applied to the HYBRIT shaft-type pilot facility in Luleå after comparison with field measurements and analytical solutions.
- Reported model behavior:
  - pressure evolution is nearly uniform and linear, while temperature response is heterogeneous and nonlinear;
  - injection produces cavern-scale vortices and withdrawal is dominated by upward forced convection;
  - the shaft-cavern junction is identified as the critical structural region;
  - modeled hydrogen-embrittlement susceptibility is highest in the shaft tubing and lowest along the cavern sidewall.
- Verification boundary:
  - the abstract says the framework was validated against field measurements and analytical solutions, but the accessible discovery record does not expose the underlying measurement series or validation residuals;
  - the risk pattern is a preprint model result, not an operator-confirmed damage observation or failure report at the HYBRIT pilot;
  - no peer-reviewed version was found in the current search.
