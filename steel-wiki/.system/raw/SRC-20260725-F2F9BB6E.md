# voestalpine 2025/26 annual report SuSteel status

voestalpine's 2025/26 annual report states that the SuSteel test plant at
voestalpine Stahl Donawitz is currently conducting trial work on producing
crude steel from hydrogen plasma in one process step.

The company says the test plant has operated successfully since 2021. In the
2025/26 business year, research priorities included securing hydrogen supply
and improving gas recovery. SuSteel-related R&D expense was EUR 0.86 million,
compared with EUR 0.89 million in 2024/25.

The report treats SuSteel as a breakthrough technology for the later stage of
voestalpine's net-zero pathway. Successful test-plant operation does not
establish continuous 200 kg/h operation or commercial readiness.
