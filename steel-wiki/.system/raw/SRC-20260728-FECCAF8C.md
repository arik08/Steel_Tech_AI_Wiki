# Figueiredo et al. (2026) — in-flight hydrogen-plasma reduction notes

- Primary article: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2589152926000104
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtla.2026.102658
- Journal: *Materialia*, volume 45, March 2026, article 102658.
- Authors: Jordan N. Figueiredo, Bassam B. Dally, Deanna A. Lacoste.
- Funding disclosed: King Abdullah University of Science and Technology baseline fund BAS/1/1396-01-01.
- Cross-check: DOI, volume/article number and authors agree between ScienceDirect and the bibliographic ResearchGate record.

## Reported results

- Stable elongated-arc, undiluted-hydrogen plasma with particles reduced in flight.
- Feedstocks: pure hematite, combusted iron from the TU Eindhoven MC² burner, and aluminium-bearing goethite.
- Residence time: 18-30 ms.
- Absolute reduction degree:
  - hematite and combusted iron: 71-76%
  - aluminium-bearing goethite: 42%
- Reported efficiency: 26.8 g-iron/kWh.
- The authors associate the lower goethite result with iron-aluminium spinel formation and state that gangue is not removed by this route.

## Evidence boundary

- The authors explicitly state that the apparatus was not designed for process-performance testing.
- The figures are feedstock-specific laboratory in-flight results, not a continuous industrial throughput, electricity-per-tonne guarantee, metal recovery yield, product specification, or H2PlasmaRed/SuSteel project KPI.
- The “efficiency” unit is recovered/reduced iron mass per input energy in this experiment; it is not an overall plant energy balance.
