Source evidence excerpt collected 2026-07-25.

Kim, Kang, and Yi investigated direct iron production from iron oxides and ores
in a B2O3-Na2O molten oxide electrolyte at 1173 K (900 degrees Celsius), far
below conventional liquid-iron MOE temperatures. Electrolysis used a 1.6 V cell
voltage in air or argon.

For reagent iron oxides, current efficiency was 65.50-68.72% in air and
67.49-78.56% in argon; the authors attribute the higher argon result to
suppression of reduced-iron reoxidation at lower oxygen partial pressure.
Tests using hematite, goethite, and magnetite ores in air produced metallic iron
at current efficiencies of 64.14%, 66.00%, and 67.58%, respectively.

This is a laboratory feasibility result rather than an industrial continuous
cell. The paper was published as an unedited early-access manuscript on
7 July 2026. Funding included Korean government programs and POSCO project
2022Z001.

Citation: Joongseok Kim, Jungshin Kang, Kyung-Woo Yi, Scientific Reports
(2026). DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-58521-y.
