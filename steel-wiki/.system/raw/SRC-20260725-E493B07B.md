# European Commission — ULCOS TGR-BF final report

Official EU Publications page:
https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/0371a7f6-3e10-40aa-a8ec-16c1bfabdc38/language-en

Released: 2014-01-31.
DOI: 10.2777/59481.

The final report states that three campaigns were run in the LKAB experimental
blast furnace in ULCOS-BF mode. A VPSA unit removed CO2, and up to 90% of the
top gas could be treated and recycled. The closed-loop operation used
conventional sinter, pellets and coke. The selected industrial configuration
was hot decarbonated top-gas injection at both hearth and shaft tuyeres.

These are experimental-blast-furnace results. They do not establish a
full-scale BF linked to permanent geological storage.
