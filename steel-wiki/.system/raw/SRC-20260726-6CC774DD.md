# Electric Smelting Furnace-Based Flowsheets

Official presentation material published by AIST for the conference
“Scrap Supplements and Alternative Ironmaking 10,” held in Orlando, Florida,
9–11 March 2026.

S. Ge (Gesa), K. Joubarani, K. Chomyn, and M. Estabrooks of Hatch present
electric-smelting-furnace flowsheets for lower-grade iron ores. The
presentation describes the ESF as a route that can reject gangue into a
low-FeO slag after pre-reduction. It also presents a vendor concept labelled
CRISP+, with an indicative electrical load of about 120–160 MW and capacity
above 1.5 Mt/y.

The scale and power figures are conceptual engineering values in a conference
presentation. They are not evidence that a named commercial plant has reached
that capacity or performance.
