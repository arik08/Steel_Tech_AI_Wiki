# Routes to reducing emissions from steel production

Verified source: Nature Reviews Clean Technology, volume 1, pages 890–902,
published 2025-12-03.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s44359-025-00118-1
Official article: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44359-025-00118-1

Authors: Claire Davis, Zushu Li, Peter Styring, Richard Curry,
Peter J. Holliman.

The review states that no single route fits every region because economics and
emission reductions depend on scrap availability and quality, ore quality,
low-carbon electricity, hydrogen, logistics, and policy.

It groups the transition into increased EAF and scrap use, lower-emission
blast-furnace measures including carbon capture and alternative reductants,
and alternative primary-iron routes such as hydrogen DRI, smelting reduction,
and iron-oxide electrolysis.

Evidence boundary: figures and route comparisons are review-level synthesis;
they must not be treated as measured performance of a specific project.
