# JFE Steel — carbon-recycling blast furnace strategy

Official strategy PDF:
https://www.jfe-steel.co.jp/en/company/pdf/carbon-neutral-strategy_220901_1.pdf

Published: 2022-09-01.

JFE's carbon-recycling BF concept separates CO2 from BF gas, reacts it with
hydrogen through methanation (CO2 + 4H2 -> CH4 + 2H2O), and reinjects the
synthetic methane as a reducing agent. The carbon therefore circulates within
the process; external low-carbon hydrogen and electricity determine the
resulting emissions.

The 2022 roadmap planned a 150 m3 Chiba test BF, trial operation from April
2025 through FY2026, and a target of at least 50% CO2 reduction versus a
conventional BF. A later stage envisioned up to a 700 m3 Kurashiki
demonstration by 2030, with post-stage-gate plans explicitly marked as
provisional.

This is an announced development roadmap and must not be presented as
commercial-scale verified performance.
