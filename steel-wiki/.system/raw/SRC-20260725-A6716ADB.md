# ArcelorMittal and John Cockerill develop low-temperature iron electrolysis

ArcelorMittal and John Cockerill announced on 2023-06-14 that they would
develop an industrial-scale low-temperature iron electrolysis plant under the
Volteron name, building on the completed EU-funded SIDERWIN project.

The companies describe Volteron as a carbon-free cold direct electrolysis
process that extracts iron from iron ore using electricity. They state that a
pilot plant produced iron plates using standard iron ore. The announcement is
evidence of pilot validation and industrial-scale development planning, not
completed commercial operation.
