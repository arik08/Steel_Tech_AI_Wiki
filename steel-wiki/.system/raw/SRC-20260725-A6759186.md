# POSCO HyREX electric smelting furnace pilot status

At the 2024 World Climate Industry Expo, POSCO described HyREX as a process
that reduces iron-ore fines in fluidized-bed reactors and melts the resulting
direct reduced iron in an electric smelting furnace.

POSCO stated that it activated a pilot electric melting furnace with a
capacity of one tonne per hour in April 2024 and produced its first molten
iron. It planned subsequent trial melting of direct reduced iron and hot
briquetted iron. This is pilot-scale evidence and does not establish
commercial-scale operation.

POSCO also stated that it was preparing the HyREX demonstration plant in
connection with a Korean government R&D program and targeted completion of
commercialization-technology development by 2030.
