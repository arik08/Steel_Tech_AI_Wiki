# Boston Metal 2026 — current MOE Steel roadmap wording

- Page: MOE Steel
- Publisher: Boston Metal
- URL: https://www.bostonmetal.com/green-steel-solution/
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Source type: current company technology page

## Current wording

- The company says its 2025 multi-inert-anode industrial cell produced tonnage
  steel and presents that commissioning run as a scale-up milestone.
- The same current page now distinguishes the near-term businesses: it says
  MOE Critical Metals will generate revenue in 2026, while MOE Steel will be
  deployed “in the coming years.”
- The page retains the technical proposition of converting iron ore grades
  directly to liquid metal with renewable electricity and oxygen as the
  intended anode product.

## Interpretation boundary

- “In the coming years” is a company roadmap phrase, not a dated FID,
  construction, license award, nameplate-capacity or commercial-operation
  commitment.
- The wording supports the independent 2026 assessment that critical metals is
  the near-term commercialization priority and that the old 2026 steel
  commercialization target should not be treated as current.
- The page does not resolve whether the announced Woburn layoffs occurred in
  full, nor does it disclose continuous campaign duration, annualized output,
  current efficiency, anode life, electricity intensity or customer
  qualification.
