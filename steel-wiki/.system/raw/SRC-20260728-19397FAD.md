# ExxonMobil 2026 climate report - Nucor Convent CCS status

- Title: Advancing Climate Solutions 2026
- Publisher: Exxon Mobil Corporation
- Published: 2026-05
- URL: https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/-/media/global/files/advancing-climate-solutions/2026/2026-advancing-climate-solutions-report.pdf
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Relevant PDF pages: 5, 16-17

## Verified status

- ExxonMobil reports approximately **9 million metric tonnes per year** of
  third-party CO2 under contract across its Gulf Coast portfolio.
- It identifies Nucor's Convent, Louisiana project as an announced CCS project
  under a long-term commercial agreement for capture, transport and permanent
  storage of up to approximately **800,000 metric tonnes of CO2 per year**.
- In the same report ExxonMobil names the CF Industries and New Generation Gas
  Gathering projects as the two projects then transporting and storing CO2.
  Nucor is not listed among those two operating projects.

## Interpretation boundary

- The May 2026 report reconfirms the Nucor contract and design volume but does
  not report capture start-up, pipeline transport, injection or measured annual
  storage for the Nucor project.
- The absence of Nucor from the two operating projects is evidence that
  operation was not confirmed in this report; it is not proof that no later
  start-up occurred after publication.
- The project concerns CCS attached to Nucor's natural-gas DRI site. It is not
  evidence of blast-furnace CCS or hydrogen conversion of the DRI unit.
