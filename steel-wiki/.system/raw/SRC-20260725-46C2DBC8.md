# Nippon Steel Kimitsu COURSE50 large blast furnace status in May 2026

Nippon Steel reported on 2026-05-29 that it was still advancing installation
and engineering work for a fiscal-2026 hydrogen-reduction demonstration at the
Kimitsu No. 2 blast furnace. The planned demonstration uses coke-oven gas and
hydrogen gas in the operating 4,500 cubic metre blast furnace.

This update confirms that the large-blast-furnace demonstration remained in
equipment-introduction and preparation status in May 2026. It does not confirm
that the demonstration had already started in January 2026. The original
January 2026 target and the later fiscal-2026 preparation status therefore
need to be shown separately in the project chronology.
