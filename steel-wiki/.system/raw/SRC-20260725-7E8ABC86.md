# China Baowu Zhanjiang near-zero-carbon line

China's State-owned Assets Supervision and Administration Commission reported that a
one-million-tonne near-zero-carbon production line had been fully commissioned at
Baoshan Iron & Steel's Zhanjiang plant, a flagship China Baowu subsidiary. The line uses
a hydrogen-based metallurgical and electric-smelting process for short-route production
of high-grade steel plate. The announcement calls the line near-zero-carbon, but the
captured page does not disclose the operating hydrogen carbon intensity or utilization.
