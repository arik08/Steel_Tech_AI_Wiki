# LKAB Gällivare HYBRIT demonstration schedule

LKAB stated on 2025-03-18 that the environmental-permit case for its
Malmberget and Gällivare operations included a first demonstration plant for
fossil-free sponge iron using HYBRIT technology.

The company described completion in 2028 and ramp-up during 2029 as its hope,
and explicitly said the process was taking longer than initially expected.
This is a company planning schedule, not evidence of construction completion
or operating production.

LKAB estimated that the demonstration plant would require approximately
5 TWh/year of additional electricity before 2030, mainly for hydrogen
production. This is a project-level estimate, not a measured operating value.
