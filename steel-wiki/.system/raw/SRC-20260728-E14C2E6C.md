# Swedish Courts — Malmberget environmental permit judgment M 1413-23

- Original: https://www.domstol.se/nyheter/2026/06/lkab-far-tillstand-till-gruvverksamheten-i-malmberget/
- Publisher: Umeå District Court, Land and Environment Court / Swedish Courts
- Published: 2026-06-15
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Case: M 1413-23

## Verified judgment scope

- The court granted LKAB an environmental permit for continued and expanded mining and processing operations in Malmberget, including a Natura 2000 permit.
- The permit includes new facilities for apatite concentrate production and a direct-reduction plant capable of producing up to 1.5 million tonnes of fossil-free sponge iron.
- The judgment attaches 93 conditions covering matters including the residential environment, blasting vibration, seismic activity, ground deformation, surface and groundwater, the Natura 2000 area, reindeer husbandry, air emissions, risk management, dam safety, remediation and monitoring.
- The court granted LKAB's request for an enforceability order.
- The partial judgment could be appealed to the Land and Environment Court of Appeal within three weeks.

## Interpretation boundary

- The permit establishes legal authorization subject to conditions; it is not an investment decision, construction start, commissioning result or confirmation that the proposed capacity will be built.
- The enforceability order means the existence of an appeal should not automatically be described as a total construction prohibition without checking the appellate court's subsequent orders.
