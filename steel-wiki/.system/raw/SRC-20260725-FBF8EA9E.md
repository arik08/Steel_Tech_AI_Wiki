# Western Australia parliamentary answer on NeoSmelt funding and FID

In a 14 May 2026 answer supplied by the Western Australian Minister for State
Development, the government stated that a final decision on the NeoSmelt pilot
was expected by the end of 2026. It also stated that conditions for release of
the A$75 million state contribution remained conditional and subject to
negotiation between the parties.

The statement confirms that, as of that date, the final investment decision
had not yet been taken and the state funding was not unconditional.
