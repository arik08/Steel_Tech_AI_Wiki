# voestalpine SuSteel hydrogen plasma smelting research

voestalpine announced on 2022-04-27 that the SuSteel project was researching
hydrogen plasma for carbon-neutral steel production at a pilot facility in
Donawitz.

The process uses hydrogen plasma in a direct-current electric arc furnace to
reduce iron ore and melt it in one process step. The company described the
project as research into a new route rather than an industrially proven
production process.

The announcement provides evidence of an operating research and pilot
facility. It does not demonstrate industrial-scale throughput, economics or
long-duration operation.
