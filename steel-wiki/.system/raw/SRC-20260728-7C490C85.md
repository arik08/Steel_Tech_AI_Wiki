# Hy4Smelt official project page — image and process notes

- Official project title: Hydrogen-based fluidised bed direct reduction of ultra-fine iron ores and smelting to green hot metal.
- The industrial-scale demonstrator combines HYFOR fluidised-bed reduction using 100% green hydrogen with a renewable-electric Smelter and further BOF processing.
- The official page states that the Smelter operates continuously under a reducing atmosphere and is intended to process high-gangue ore.
- Project timeline shown on the page: 1 April 2025 to 30 September 2029.
- The page displays a technical image captioned “Schematic illustration of a Smelter from the inside” and credits Primetals Technologies Austria GmbH.
