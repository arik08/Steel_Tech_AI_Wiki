# Sveriges Radio — Malmberget permit appealed

- Original: https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/tillstandet-for-lkabs-gruva-i-malmberget-overklagas-aven-av-lkab
- Publisher: Sveriges Radio P4 Norrbotten
- Published: 2026-07-07
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified update

- Sveriges Radio reported that the Swedish Agency for Marine and Water Management appealed the Malmberget permit.
- The report states that the agency considers the judgment insufficient to protect aquatic environments.
- The page caption states that both the agency and LKAB appealed the permit.
- The underlying permit had been granted on 2026-06-15 and includes facilities for fossil-free sponge iron and apatite concentrate.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- The 2026-06-15 Swedish Courts judgment confirms that the permit covered a direct-reduction plant, carried 93 conditions, allowed appeal within three weeks and included an enforceability order.
- The appeal is therefore a live legal and schedule-risk signal, but the sources reviewed do not establish that the permit has been stayed, revoked or that construction is prohibited.
- The appeal does not replace the separate unresolved investment-decision and financing questions for the HYBRIT demonstration plant.
