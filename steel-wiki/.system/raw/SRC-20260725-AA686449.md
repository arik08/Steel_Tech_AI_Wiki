# Nucor circular EAF steelmaking

Nucor describes its production base as electric-arc-furnace steelmaking using recycled
scrap. The current sustainability page reports 0.77 tCO2e per tonne of steel for its
circular EAF route using 2025 worldsteel figures and average recycled content of 79.1%.
Econiq certification combines EAF production with renewable or emission-free electricity
and carbon offsets. These data support low-embodied-carbon production but are not evidence
of hydrogen DRI, electric smelting or molten oxide electrolysis deployment.
