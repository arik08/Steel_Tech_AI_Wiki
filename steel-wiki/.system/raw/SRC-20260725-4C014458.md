# POSCO and Electra low-temperature iron technology partnership

POSCO announced on 2026-04-28 that it signed a joint development agreement
with Electra for low-carbon iron production technology. Electra uses an
electrochemical process to remove impurities from iron ore and produce solid
iron.

POSCO stated that Electra was constructing a demonstration plant with annual
capacity of 500 tonnes and planned to begin operation in 2026. The companies
will combine POSCO's direct-reduction and engineering technologies with
Electra's clean-iron system and jointly evaluate technical and economic
feasibility for commercial production.

POSCO Investment also announced a corporate venture investment in Electra.
This is evidence of a joint development and validation program, not evidence
that commercial-scale production has already been achieved.
