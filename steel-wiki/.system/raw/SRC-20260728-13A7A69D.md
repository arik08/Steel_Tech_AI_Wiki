## Verified relevant content

The presentation compares conventional and novel ironmaking routes from an
energy, emissions and scale-up perspective. It classifies direct
electrification routes as lower-TRL technologies under active development.

For the two U.S. electrochemical routes, the presentation reports:

- Electra: 500 t/y demonstration target in mid-2026 and a company commercial
  scale target around 2029.
- Boston Metal MOE: near-term commercial effort prioritises higher-value
  niobium/critical-metals production, while the presenter estimates a steel
  demonstration around 2028.

The Boston Metal timing is an independent conference-presenter estimate based
on a cited 2025 Canary Media report, not a Boston Metal construction
commitment. It conflicts with older company statements that a first steel
demonstration would deploy in 2026, and should therefore be retained as a
schedule-risk signal rather than silently replacing the company target.

The presentation gives a rough energy range of about 13.5-15.4 GJ/t including
20-30 pct estimated losses for direct-electrification routes. The author labels
these values as rough estimates dependent on final process design; they are not
measured Boston Metal or Electra plant performance.
