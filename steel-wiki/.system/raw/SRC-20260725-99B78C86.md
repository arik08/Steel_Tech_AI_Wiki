# Sticking in Shaft Furnace and Fluidized Bed Ironmaking Processes — evidence excerpt

Source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11663-024-03188-x

Published: 2024-07-26

Collected: 2026-07-25

## Verified statements

- The open-access review identifies sticking as a production-limiting problem
  in both shaft-furnace and fluidized-bed DRI.
- Sticking is associated with iron whiskers, freshly formed high-surface-energy
  metallic iron and low-melting eutectic phases.
- Higher reduction temperature generally increases sticking, while simply
  lowering temperature reduces reaction rate and production yield.
- Inert surface coatings can form a physical barrier and inhibit metallic-iron
  bonding, but coating chemistry, dosage, adhesion, application stage and ore
  properties affect performance.
- The review reports that around 75% of DRI was then produced in shaft
  furnaces and describes the trade-off between high-grade pellet routes and
  fines-based fluidized beds.

The paper is a literature review; individual coating test results should not be
generalised to all ores or reactors.
