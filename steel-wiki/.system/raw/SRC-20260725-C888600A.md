# Tenova for POSCO Gwangyang plant

Tenova announced on 7 June 2023 that POSCO selected its electric-arc-furnace
equipment for the Gwangyang plant after two years of co-engineering and
competitive comparison.

The announced configuration is a full-platform EAF capable of tapping 280 t of
liquid steel. It combines the Consteel continuous scrap charging and preheating
system with Consteerrer electromagnetic stirring, jointly patented by Tenova
and ABB. Tenova also listed robotic applications and the Safe+ EAF water-leak
detection system.

The supplier stated that the EAF product would be combined with liquid iron
from the existing blast-furnace route. The 2023 announcement scheduled
production for the end of 2025; later POSCO and Tenova releases document the
actual 2026 inauguration.

The original project page includes an aerial view of Gwangyang Works. Tenova's
official EAF technology page also provides equipment renderings, including a
transparent internal view of the Consteerrer installation. That generic
supplier drawing explains the installed technology but is not an as-built
drawing of POSCO's completed furnace.
