# POSCO completes Gwangyang EAF and advances HyREX

POSCO announced on 2026-06-22 that it completed a large-scale electric arc
furnace at Gwangyang Works. The facility has annual capacity of 2.5 million
tonnes and required approximately KRW 600 billion of investment. POSCO stated
that construction began in February 2024 and that the completed EAF had begun
low-carbon steel production.

POSCO said the EAF can reduce carbon emissions by up to approximately 75%
compared with its 2017-2019 blast-furnace baseline, subject to scrap supply and
the power-generation mix. It is developing hot-metal-mixing, scrap-selection,
and composition-control technologies with the aim of mass-producing premium
automotive and electrical steel sheets by 2030.

The same announcement states that POSCO is preparing the site for HyREX at
Pohang Works. POSCO plans to use a HyREX demonstration facility with annual
capacity of 300,000 tonnes to complete commercialization-technology
development by 2030. This is a company target, not evidence that commercial
HyREX operation has already been achieved.
