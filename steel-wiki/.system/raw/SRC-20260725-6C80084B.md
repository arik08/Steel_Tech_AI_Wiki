# Tata Steel climate technology roadmap

Tata Steel's roadmap covers hydrogen steelmaking, CCU, scrap EAF capacity and DRI in
the Netherlands. It reports a 5 tonne-per-day blast-furnace-gas CO2 capture pilot at
Jamshedpur and lists hydrogen steelmaking and CCU scale-up as medium-term work.
The page also records a first-in-world digital twin in sinter making and World Economic
Forum Global Lighthouse recognition for Jamshedpur, Kalinganagar and IJmuiden.
HIsarna and new smelting technologies remain in the long-term development portfolio.
