# IEA Emissions Measurement and Data Collection for a Net Zero Steel Industry

The IEA concludes that common definitions of near-zero steel require sound
measurement and robust data. A fit-for-purpose methodology should allow
comparison across all facilities, use an emissions boundary and scope compatible
with net zero, and apply compatible rules for credits and co-products.

The methodology should prefer site- and product-specific, auditable measured
data over generic estimates or emissions factors. Data collection should provide
high granularity and transparency, support regular parallel reporting under
multiple methodologies, and minimise reporting burden.

The report states that no existing framework or combination of frameworks fully
meets all proposed principles. Therefore a company-reported intensity cannot be
compared with another value until the system boundary, electricity treatment,
raw-material inputs, co-product allocation and credit rules are aligned.
