# Impact of Hydrogen DRI on EAF Steelmaking — evidence excerpt

Source: https://www.midrex.com/tech-article/impact-of-hydrogen-dri-on-eaf-steelmaking/

Collected: 2026-07-25

## Verified statements

- Replacing CO-bearing reducing gas with hydrogen makes the final FeO-to-Fe
  reduction endothermic and removes in-situ DRI carburisation.
- The article characterises the resulting product as 0% carbon DRI and warns
  that EAF heat balance, stirring, FeO reduction, slag foaming, yield and
  productivity must be redesigned or compensated.
- In current EAF practice, chemical energy supplies about 35–50% of melt
  energy. Carbon in conventional DRI can contribute high-efficiency chemical
  energy and CO for foamy slag.
- The article cites more than 300,000 tonnes of 0% carbon Circored material
  produced industrially and reports that subsequent EAF trials required
  altered operating practice and added carbon and oxygen.
- Lower-grade ore raises gangue and FeO burdens; combining such feed with
  zero-carbon DRI can increase electricity and yield penalties.

These are technology-supplier analyses and historical trial interpretations,
not universal EAF guarantees.
