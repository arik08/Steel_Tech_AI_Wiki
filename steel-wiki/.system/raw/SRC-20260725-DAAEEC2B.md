# Nippon Steel high-grade steel production in large EAFs

Nippon Steel reports that it installed an experimental electric arc furnace
with a capacity of 10 tonnes per charge to develop and verify technologies for
commercial-scale high-grade steel production in large EAFs.

The company states that it began testing high-efficiency dephosphorization and
denitrification processes in the experimental EAF in 2025. Nippon Steel also
reports commercial production of high-grade steel in its integrated EAF
steelmaking process at Hirohata.

The experimental program is intended to raise EAF productivity and quality
toward the blast-furnace and basic-oxygen-furnace route. The company report
does not establish that all high-grade products can already be made at
commercial scale from unrestricted scrap mixes.
