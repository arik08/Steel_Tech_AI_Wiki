# ArcelorMittal Dunkirk electric arc furnace

ArcelorMittal confirmed construction of a €1.3 billion electric arc furnace at Dunkirk.
The furnace is designed for 2 million tonnes per year and start-up is scheduled for 2029.
The announced metallic feed mix is scrap, HBI/DRI and hot metal. The company estimates
0.6 tonnes of CO2 per tonne of steel, about one third of a blast-furnace comparison.
French Energy Efficiency Certificates are expected to support 50% of the investment.
The decision concerns an EAF; this release does not confirm a new hydrogen DRI unit.
