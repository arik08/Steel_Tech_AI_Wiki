# Metallurgical mechanisms and prediction of EAF endpoint phosphorus

Lu and co-authors describe endpoint phosphorus as a key EAF quality variable.
Their metallurgical model represents phosphorus oxidation by FeO followed by
fixation of phosphorus oxide with CaO in the slag. The principal process
drivers are slag CaO activity and furnace oxygen potential; extending smelting
time alone cannot indefinitely improve dephosphorization after equilibrium is
approached.

The paper trains an explainable TabNet model on actual production data and
reports a mean absolute error of 0.0024, RMSE of 0.0034, and a 90.82% hit rate
inside a plus-or-minus 0.005% error band. These values describe one published
dataset and model, not a universal EAF guarantee. The work demonstrates that
charge, slag, temperature, and operating data must be interpreted together
when controlling phosphorus.
