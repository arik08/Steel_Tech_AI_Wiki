# Tata Steel EASyMelt collaboration

Tata Steel and SMS group agreed to technical work toward a joint industrial
demonstration of EASyMelt at Jamshedpur's E blast furnace. EASyMelt is an
electric-assisted syngas smelter concept using blast-furnace top-gas recycling,
coke-oven-gas reforming and plasma-torch heating. The stated objective was more
than 50% CO2 reduction from the blast-furnace baseline. The release records an
MoU and intended demonstration, not completed deployment.
