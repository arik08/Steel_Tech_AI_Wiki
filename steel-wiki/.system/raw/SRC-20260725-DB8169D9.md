# Impact of pre-reduction degree on HPSR

Ernst, Manzoor, Souza Filho, Zarl, and Schenk published this peer-reviewed
Metals article on 2023-03-10 (DOI 10.3390/met13030558).

Experiments compared pre-reduced iron ores and iron-bearing residues to
evaluate reduction behavior, hydrogen consumption, process time, and metal
microstructure. Wet ore increased electrode consumption during argon
premelting. Magnetite gave the highest reduction rate and subsequent hydrogen
conversion in the reported experiments.

Hematite showed a kinetic transition at approximately 80 to 85% reduction,
after which the reduction rate decreased. The authors report that direct
magnetite use and rapid high-temperature final reduction are possible in HPSR
and propose combining solid-state or fluidized-bed pre-reduction with plasma
finishing to improve overall gas use and process time. Results are
experimental and ore-specific.
