# JFE carbon-recycling blast furnace concept

JFE's technical report describes an oxygen blast furnace combined with CO2 separation,
external low-carbon hydrogen and methanation. Captured blast-furnace CO2 is converted
to synthetic methane and recycled as reductant. The report estimated approximately
30% lower blast-furnace CO2 generation than a conventional process. This is a technology
development concept and should not be read as evidence of commercial-scale operation.
