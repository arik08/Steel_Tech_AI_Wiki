# Rio Tinto 2025 annual report Hy4Smelt notes

- Filing: Rio Tinto Annual Report on Form 20-F for year ended 2025-12-31
- Filed: 2026-02-19
- Publisher: Rio Tinto / U.S. SEC
- URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/863064/000162828026009531/rio-20251231.htm
- Evidence type: company annual filing by a Hy4Smelt consortium member.
- Relevant disclosure:
  - Rio Tinto entered the consortium with Primetals and voestalpine to develop the Hy4Smelt industrial-scale prototype integrating fines-based HYFOR with an ESF;
  - 2026 action is to continue Hy4Smelt construction with a target to operationalize by 2027.
- Verification boundary:
  - the filing independently cross-checks the participant-reported construction status and 2027 target;
  - “target to operationalise” is a forward-looking milestone, not achieved first production or continuous-operation evidence;
  - the disclosure does not resolve the EMECR 2.6/2.4 versus 3/3 t/h design-capacity discrepancy.
