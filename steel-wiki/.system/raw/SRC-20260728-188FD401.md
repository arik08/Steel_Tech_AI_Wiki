# Kim and Jung 2026 HyREX multistage fluidized-bed experiment notes

- Title: Experimental Study on Multistage Hydrogen Reduction of Iron Ore by Fluidized Bed Reactor
- Authors: Seong-Jin Kim; Sungmo Jung
- Affiliation: Pohang University of Science and Technology
- Event: AISTech 2026, Direct Reduced Iron: Other Reduction Methods
- Presentation date: 4 May 2026
- Evidence type: official conference paper and presentation record; peer-review status not stated.
- Experimental scope:
  - lab-scale fluidized-bed experiment intended to simulate a four-stage HyREX reduction sequence;
  - 400 g of fine iron ore;
  - sequential R4–R3–R2–R1 stages using N2, H2, and H2O.
- Results reported in the official AIST record:
  - most conversion to metallic iron occurred in the R2–R1 stages;
  - reduction degree was mainly governed by temperature and gas oxidation degree;
  - the rate-controlling description shifted from three-dimensional diffusion toward a third-order reaction during wüstite-to-iron transformation;
  - the R2-stage kinetics changed with microstructural evolution from isolated nuclei to layered grains.
- Verification boundary:
  - this is a 400 g laboratory sequence, not the 300,000 t/y Pohang HyREX integrated demonstration plant;
  - it supports multistage reaction design and control interpretation, not industrial throughput, hydrogen use, availability, or metallization guarantees.
