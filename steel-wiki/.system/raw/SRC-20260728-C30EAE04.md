# MHI FY2024 transition-bond report: Ghent capture and D-CRBN demonstration

- Report date: 2025-12-05
- Reporting boundary: allocation status as of 2025-03-31 and FY2024 impact reporting.
- MHI states that it installed its CO2 capture unit at ArcelorMittal Gent and conducted a demonstration test covering steelworks CO2 capture and reuse.
- The stated route captured CO2 from blast-furnace off-gas and hot-strip-mill reheating-furnace off-gas, then converted captured CO2 to carbon monoxide using D-CRBN plasma technology.
- MHI described potential downstream uses for the CO as a blast-furnace reductant or Steelanol/chemical/alternative-fuel feedstock.
- Cross-check: DNV's second-party periodic review dated the same day independently records that MHI conducted the Gent demonstration test.
- Disclosure limit: neither report provides conversion rate, CO production, product-gas purity, electric-energy intensity, run hours, or a scale-up decision. “Conducted a demonstration test” must not be read as commercial validation or continuous operation.
