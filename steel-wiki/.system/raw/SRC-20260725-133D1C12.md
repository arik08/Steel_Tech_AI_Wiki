Source evidence excerpt collected 2026-07-25 from ARPA-E ROSIE project
descriptions.

ARPA-E lists Electra's "Low-Temperature Green Ironmaking from Unconventional
Feedstocks" project with an award amount of $2,874,596. The described process
uses two electrochemical cell stacks. The first is an acid-base generator that
uses electricity to produce sulfuric acid and sodium hydroxide for feedstock
processing and separation, including ferrous hydroxide. The second is an
electrowinning cell that converts ferrous hydroxide into iron metal.

The document describes 80% lower greenhouse-gas emissions and half the cost of
existing fossil-fuel processes as conditional project outcomes ("If
successful"), not achieved results.
