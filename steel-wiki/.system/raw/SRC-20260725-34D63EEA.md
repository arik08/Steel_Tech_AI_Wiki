# Austrian Climate and Energy Fund SuS-F record

The Austrian Climate and Energy Fund records SuS-F Sustainable Steelmaking
Follow Up under the Energy Flagship Region program, administered by FFG, with
voestalpine Stahl as the funding applicant.

The current government page lists the project period as 2022-10-01 through
2026-09-30, funding of EUR 3,946,740, and total project volume of EUR
6,770,925. This end date is one year later than the 2025-09-30 date still
shown on K1-MET's project page and should be treated as the current government
record rather than silently overwriting the earlier published schedule.

The project studies integration of hydrogen-plasma reduction technology into
existing steel production locations. Listed partners are K1-MET, Pirhofer
Automation, Montanuniversitaet Leoben, voestalpine Stahl Donawitz,
voestalpine Stahl, and WIVA P&G.
