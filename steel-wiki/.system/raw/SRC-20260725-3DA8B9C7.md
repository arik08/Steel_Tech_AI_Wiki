# Sortera Technologies AI sensor sorting platform

Sortera states that its patented AI-based sensor sorting platform is used in
automotive metals recycling to upgrade feedstock streams and remove unwanted
contaminants before the upgraded streams are used in new products.

The public technology page shows a generic process illustration: end-of-life
products are shredded, mixed scrap passes through an AI sorting machine, and
sorted specification scrap becomes feedstock for new products. The page
describes the company's established metals-sorting platform and government
project history, mainly in automotive and light-metal recycling.

This page does not depict the as-built DOE-funded steel-scrap copper-removal
module and does not prove that the thermomechanical decoppering project has
entered commercial steel-scrap operation.
