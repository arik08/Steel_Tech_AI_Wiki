# Yu et al. 2026 — acidic iron electrowinning hydrogen evolution

- Title: Hydrogen evolution behavior and morphology regulation of
  electrowinning of iron in acidic sulfate system
- Journal: Journal of Cleaner Production, volume 550, article 147910
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2026.147910
- Authors: Ruijie Yu; Jiaxin Cheng; Xin Wang; Haitao Yang; Qingping Chai;
  Xiaoyun Chen; Xiaogang Lin; Tongtong Zhang
- Published: 2026-03-15
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Peer review: journal research article

## Reported mechanism and controls

- In acidic ferrous-sulfate electrowinning, competing hydrogen evolution
  lowers iron-deposition current efficiency and degrades deposit uniformity.
- The authors attribute pores and microcrack initiation/propagation to the
  bubble sequence of evolution, growth, coalescence and desorption.
- Temperature adjustment, cathode surface sandblasting and modification of the
  current-deposition mode were tested or proposed together to improve bubble
  release and deposit morphology.

## Interpretation boundary

- Improved morphology in the reported electrochemical tests is not evidence of
  continuous ore leaching, electrolyte purification, industrial plate
  stripping, long electrode life, product qualification or plant economics.
- The paper describes industrial potential; it does not report a commercial or
  integrated demonstration plant.
