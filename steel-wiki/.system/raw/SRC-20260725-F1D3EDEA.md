# Rio Tinto BioIron research and development facility

Rio Tinto announced on 2024-06-04 that it would invest USD 143 million to
develop a BioIron research and development facility in Western Australia.

BioIron combines iron ore and raw biomass and uses microwave energy to produce
metallic iron. Rio Tinto states that the new facility will include a pilot
plant ten times larger than the preceding small-scale pilot in Germany. The
company claims potential emissions reductions of up to 95 percent compared
with the blast-furnace route when renewable energy and sustainable biomass
carbon circulation are used.

The investment and scale-up are confirmed company actions. The emissions
reduction figure is a company estimate subject to energy, biomass and system
boundary assumptions, and the announcement does not prove commercial-scale
operation.
