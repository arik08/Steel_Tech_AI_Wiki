# POSCO ESG page — HyREX demonstration schedule

POSCO's current climate-change page states that final government approval for
development of the 1.35 million-square-metre hydrogen-reduction steelmaking
site at Pohang Works was obtained in March 2026.

The page describes a 300,000-tonne-per-year HyREX demonstration plant,
scheduled to break ground in April 2026 and targeted for completion in 2028.
It states that phased trial operation through 2030 is intended to establish
optimal operating conditions and raise technical maturity for commercial
facilities. The April construction statement is a schedule on the page; an
independent or later confirmation of actual groundbreaking is still required.

POSCO also records a 50 kg/batch hydrogen fluidised-reduction test furnace
introduced in 2023 and a 1 t/h electric smelting furnace completed in April
2024 with first molten-iron tapping. Those unit-operation results do not prove
continuous operation of the 300,000 t/y integrated HyREX facility.
