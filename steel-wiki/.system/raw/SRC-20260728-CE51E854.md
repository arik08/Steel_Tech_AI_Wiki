# Boston Metal Woburn layoff delay after interim funding - Patch notes

- Publisher: Woburn Patch
- Author: William Hall
- Published and updated: 2026-03-13
- URL: https://patch.com/massachusetts/woburn/expecting-layoffs-saturday-71-boston-metal-works-get-reprieve-now
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified report

Patch reported that Boston Metal obtained limited new funding and delayed the
planned reduction of 71 Woburn employees from March 14 to April 21, 2026. The
article cites an updated state notice and describes the extension as temporary.

The report does not confirm that the April reduction was completed, that the
full Woburn research programme resumed, or that the funding restored the MOE
steel demonstration schedule. Later company financing must be tracked
separately by stated use of proceeds.
