# Reduction Behaviour of Iron Ores by H2 at Multi-Stage Reduction

Official presentation material published by AIST for the conference
“Scrap Supplements and Alternative Ironmaking 10,” held in Orlando, Florida,
9–11 March 2026. The presentation is dated 11 March 2026.

Seongkyu Cho, Seong-Jin Kim, Leonardo Tomas da Rocha, and Sung-Mo Jung examine
multi-stage hydrogen reduction and ore-dependent behavior. For four ores, the
presentation reports final reduction degrees of 89% for Carajás, 80% for MAC,
81% for Roy Hill, and 73.3% for Yandi under the stated test sequence. It
concludes that final reduction degree decreased as gangue content increased
and that gangue can promote difficult-to-reduce phases such as fayalite.

The presentation also notes that fluidized-bed routes such as FINEX and HyREX
can use fine ore without pelletizing or sintering, while requiring relatively
large hydrogen flow for fluidization. The experimental results are
conference-presented laboratory evidence and are not direct proof of
industrial HyREX performance.
