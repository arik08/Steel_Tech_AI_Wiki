# Lane et al. 2026 Australian ore H2 fluidized-bed notes

- Title: Hydrogen fluidised-bed reduction of Australian hematite-goethite iron ores
- Authors: Daniel J. Lane; Bavinesh Maisuria; Sarah Spencer; Ben Rumsey; Sameer Usmani; John Pye; Alireza Rahbari; Keith R. Vining; Patrick G. Hartley; Matthew Watson; Chris W. Bumby
- Venue: Cleaner Engineering and Technology 32 (2026), 101212
- DOI: 10.1016/j.clet.2026.101212
- Published: June 2026
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed journal article.
- Experimental scope:
  - four Pilbara hematite-goethite ores containing 57–61 wt% Fe;
  - air calcination at 500°C, followed by batch hydrogen fluidized-bed reduction from 500°C to 900°C.
- Results:
  - stable fluidization for all tested ores from 500°C to 800°C;
  - at 900°C, three of four ores showed sticking, gas channelling, and bed defluidization;
  - reduction degree exceeded 93% within 12 min at 700°C and 18 min at 800°C;
  - fayalite formation from silicon-bearing impurities limited final reduction degree at 700–800°C;
  - wüstite reduction became rate-limiting at high temperature.
- Verification boundary:
  - this is independent batch-scale ore testing, not a HYFOR or HyREX integrated pilot campaign;
  - it supports the general temperature/sticking and mineralogy constraints for fine-ore hydrogen fluidized beds, not a commercial throughput or availability guarantee.
