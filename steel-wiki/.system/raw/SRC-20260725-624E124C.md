# ArcelorMittal Europe — first industrial ethanol from Steelanol

Official release:
https://europe.arcelormittal.com/newsandmedia/pressreleases/6227/Steelanol-first-industrial-scale-production

Published: 2023-11-16.
Milestone date: 2023-11-07.

ArcelorMittal reported first industrial-scale ethanol production at the Ghent
Steelanol plant on 7 November 2023. The company described the milestone as a
step toward full commissioning. The plant biologically converts carbon-rich
steel-production gases to ethanol using LanzaTech technology.
