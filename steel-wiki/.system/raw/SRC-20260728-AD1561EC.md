# Hamuyuni et al. 2025 Metso DRI smelting slag-window notes

- Title: Fluxing Options and Slag Operating Window for Metso’s Sustainable DRI Smelting Furnace
- Authors: Joseph Hamuyuni; Fiseha Tesfaye; Kimmo Vallo; Timo Haimi; Jouni Pihlasalo; Mari Lindgren
- Venue: JOM 77(5), 3446–3456
- Published online: 2025-02-24
- DOI: 10.1007/s11837-025-07243-z
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed journal article; all authors are affiliated with Metso, so it is supplier-authored evidence.
- Technical scope:
  - proposes fluxing and temperature windows for a six-in-line DRI smelting furnace intended to treat BF-grade/high-gangue DRI;
  - reports an optimal operating-temperature range of 1300–1600°C for the examined slag system;
  - for the examined compositions, slag viscosity at 1500°C was below 0.35 Pa·s;
  - experimental refractory-compatibility work found magnesia-chromite more compatible for the studied slag compositions and temperature range.
- Verification boundary:
  - the values describe the examined slag chemistry and supplier-developed furnace concept;
  - they are not universal feedstock guarantees and are not disclosed aggregate performance data from the Pori customer campaigns;
  - supplier affiliation remains relevant even though the article is peer reviewed.
