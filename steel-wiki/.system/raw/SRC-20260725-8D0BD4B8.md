# thyssenkrupp Steel Duisburg DR plant status

The project site reports that main construction began in late 2024 and SMS started
erecting the DR tower in February 2026. Commissioning is now stated to begin in 2027.
The plant is designed for 2.5 million tonnes of DRI and includes downstream innovative
melters integrated with the existing steelworks. Ramp-up will begin with natural gas,
then progressively increase hydrogen toward 100%; regional hydrogen-network connection
is planned by 2027. The company estimates potential avoidance of over 3.5 Mt CO2 per year
when hydrogen is used.
