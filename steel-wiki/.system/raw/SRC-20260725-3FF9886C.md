# China Baowu hydrogen DRI and electric-smelting trials

China Baowu and Rio Tinto completed industrial-scale pelletisation and shaft-furnace
trials at Zhanjiang. Pellets containing one-third Pilbara Blend ore were reduced in a
1 Mtpa hydrogen-based shaft furnace. The DRI was converted in an industrial BOF and
also tested in a 500 kg electric smelting furnace. The result supports use of mid-grade
ore in hydrogen DRI and potential combination with electric smelting, but the electric
smelting test itself remained small scale.
