# Tomorrow’s Steel Mill, Today: Ternium Pesquería Leads Sustainable Innovation

Official presentation material published by AIST for the conference
“Scrap Supplements and Alternative Ironmaking 10,” held in Orlando, Florida,
9–11 March 2026.

Fadi Abularage of Tenova HYL and Kyle Shoop of Tenova describe the Pesquería,
Nuevo León development. The presentation identifies Phase 3 as in progress and
shows an integrated DRI–EAF–LF–RH-OB production line. It lists 2.1 Mt/y of DRI,
1.5 Mt/y of scrap, and 2.6 Mt/y of slabs, and describes the DRI unit as a
2.1 Mt/y HDRI/CDRI ENERGIRON plant.

This is an official conference presentation from equipment suppliers involved
in the project, not a peer-reviewed paper. The capacities and construction
status are supplier-reported as of the conference material and require
project-owner or operating evidence before being treated as achieved output.
