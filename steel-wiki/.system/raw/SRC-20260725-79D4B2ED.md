# IEA Energy Technology Perspectives 2026 — executive summary

The IEA reported that 105 Mt of near-zero-emissions steel capacity had been
announced since 2020, approximately 5% of current production and roughly twice
the conventional capacity added over the same period. Only 5% of that announced
near-zero capacity had reached final investment decision.

The IEA also classified iron-ore electrolysis among technologies still at early
readiness with substantial technical and cost barriers. These technologies may
be transformative but are unlikely to reach significant market shares within a
decade.

This evidence supports separating announcement, credible conversion plan, FID,
construction, commissioning, ramp-up and verified production rather than treating
all announced capacity as operating low-carbon capacity.
