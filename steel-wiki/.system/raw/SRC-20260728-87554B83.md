# Belga 2025 — Steelanol closure risk and regulatory economics

- Title: Europe puts flagship green project in jeopardy: ArcelorMittal considers shutting down the Steelanol plant in Ghent
- Publisher: Belga News Agency
- Published: 2025-06-18
- URL: https://www.belganewsagency.eu/europe-puts-flagship-green-project-in-jeopardy-arcelormittal-considers-shutting-down-the-steelanol-plant-in-ghent
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified operating and business-risk signals

- ArcelorMittal Belgium CEO Frederik Van de Velde told Belga that the company
  would give itself about one year to decide whether to close Steelanol.
- He acknowledged unresolved technical issues and said that even if the plant
  reached its planned 60,000 t/y ethanol output, economics would remain
  difficult without regulatory changes.
- The article reports that the ethanol was not recognised under EU rules as
  either an advanced biofuel or a recycled fuel, and that the avoided emissions
  could not be deducted from ArcelorMittal's carbon balance.
- Belga reports EUR 215 million invested in Steelanol, EUR 250 million together
  with Torero, and 35 Steelanol employees.

## Interpretation boundary

- This is evidence of a conditional closure review announced in June 2025, not
  evidence that Steelanol was closed.
- The technical and economic statements are attributed to ArcelorMittal's local
  CEO. Belga independently records the statement but does not publish audited
  production, yield, uptime or cost data.
- The 60,000 t/y and avoided-emissions figures are targets, not demonstrated
  annual output.
