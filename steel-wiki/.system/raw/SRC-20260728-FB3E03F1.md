# European Commission answer on Steelanol RCF methodology

- Document: Answer given by Commissioner Dan Jørgensen on behalf of the European Commission
- Reference: P-002752/2025
- Answer date: 2025-09-17
- The Commission described Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/1185 as the methodology for lifecycle greenhouse-gas savings of renewable fuels of non-biological origin and recycled carbon fuels.
- It stated that a delegated regulation adopted on 2025-07-08 changed treatment of electricity-emission intensity and rigid-input displacement.
- The Commission said those changes could be taken into account when Regulation 2023/1185 is aligned with the revised Renewable Energy Directive.
- The Commission expected the changes to make the greenhouse-gas-savings threshold easier to meet for recycled-carbon-fuel installations using unavoidable industrial off-gases, explicitly naming Steelanol.
- Boundary: the answer describes an expected regulatory facilitation, not completed recognition of Steelanol ethanol or proof of commercial viability.
