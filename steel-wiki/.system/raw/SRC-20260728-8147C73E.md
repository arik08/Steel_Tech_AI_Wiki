# Stegra — Final electrolyzer module installed at Boden

- Original: https://stegra.com/en/news-and-stories/final-electrolyzer-module-installed-at-stegras-green-hydrogen-plant
- Publisher: Stegra
- Published: 2026-04-02
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified construction milestone

- Stegra announced installation of the final electrolyzer module at its Boden green-hydrogen plant.
- The company explicitly stated that additional work remained before the hydrogen plant would be complete.
- The milestone therefore verifies module installation, not completed balance of plant, commissioning, hydrogen output or integrated DRI operation.

## Cross-check

- A Swedish government release on 2026-06-08 described the Boden steelworks as under construction and documented a ministerial visit after inauguration of the rail connection to Boden Industrial Park.
- SVT's 2026-06-11 site report separately documented ongoing construction, reduced staffing versus the 2025 peak and a DRI tower at about 120 m.
