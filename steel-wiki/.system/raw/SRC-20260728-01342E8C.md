# Boston Metal raises USD 75 million for critical-metals business

- Publisher: Boston Metal via GlobeNewswire
- Published: 2026-05-20
- URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/20/3298554/0/en/boston-metal-raises-75-million-to-scale-critical-metals-business.html
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified company announcement

Boston Metal announced USD 75 million of new capital, bringing its stated total
funding above USD 500 million. Tata Steel joined the round with existing
investors.

The stated use of proceeds is expansion of the critical-metals business and
additional commercial-scale deployments for niobium, tantalum, vanadium and
nickel in the United States and globally. The announcement describes critical
metals as the company's first commercial market because of nearer-term demand
and economics.

The release does not allocate the USD 75 million to a Woburn MOE Steel
demonstration plant, does not provide a restored Woburn steel-project
construction schedule, and does not report commercial steel production. It
therefore confirms ongoing corporate financing and MOE platform activity while
also confirming that the immediate commercial focus is critical metals.

The attached Boston Metal MOE diagram is a platform-level selective-recovery
diagram for critical metals. It is not a steel-cell section drawing and is not
more suitable as the Woburn steel-project representative image than the
existing official multi-inert-anode cell photograph.
