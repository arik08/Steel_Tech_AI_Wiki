# Svenska kraftnät Degerträsk / Stegra grid notes

- Page: Station Degerträsk driftsatt
- Publisher: Svenska kraftnät
- Published: 2025-10-30
- URL: https://www.svk.se/utveckling-av-kraftsystemet/transmissionsnatet/transmissionsnatsprojekt/degertrask/nytt-om-station-degertrask/station-degertrask-driftsatt/
- Evidence type: Swedish transmission-system operator project update.
- Confirmed milestones:
  - the new 400 kV Degerträsk station was energized and placed in service on schedule;
  - it was built because Stegra’s Boden steel and hydrogen facilities required a new national-grid connection;
  - further works continued in 2026 to enable additional power withdrawal.
- Regional scope:
  - the cited approximately 2,000 MW potential increased regional withdrawal in 2028–2030 depends on multiple ongoing and planned line/station projects, not solely on the commissioned station or solely on Stegra.
- Verification boundary:
  - station energization confirms a major grid-connection milestone;
  - it does not prove that Stegra’s electrolyzers, DRI plant, EAF, or rolling mill are commissioned or receiving their full design load.
