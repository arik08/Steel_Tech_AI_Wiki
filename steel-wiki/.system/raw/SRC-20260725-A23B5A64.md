# HYFOR hydrogen-based fine-ore reduction

Primetals Technologies describes HYFOR as a hydrogen direct-reduction route
for iron-ore concentrate in which 100% of the particles may be smaller than
0.15 mm. The material is not indurated into conventional pellets.

The process has three main areas: preheating and oxidation, gas purification,
and a cascade of fluidized-bed reduction reactors. Fine concentrate is heated
above 800 degrees Celsius before reduction. Hot DRI leaves the reduction
reactor at about 600 degrees Celsius and may be cooled, hot-briquetted, or
passed to downstream melting.

The supplier presents the process as modular and claims potential reductions
in primary energy use and carbon emissions. Those values are design aims and
depend on the hydrogen and electricity supply, ore beneficiation, material
yield, and downstream melting boundary.

The official page documents actual HYFOR pilot-plant equipment in Austria,
including the material bins, gas and solids pipework, and the fluidized-bed
reactor. It also states that the HY4SMELT project is validating the combination
of HYFOR with an electric smelter.
