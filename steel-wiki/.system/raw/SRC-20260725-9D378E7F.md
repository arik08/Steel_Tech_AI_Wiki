# ArcelorMittal investment in molten oxide electrolysis

ArcelorMittal invested $36 million in Boston Metal through the XCarb Innovation Fund.
Boston Metal is developing Molten Oxide Electrolysis, an electrically powered one-step
route from iron ore to liquid metal. The release described modular cells, broad ore-grade
compatibility and no process CO2 from the electrolysis step. This is a strategic equity
investment in an external technology developer, not evidence of an ArcelorMittal-owned
commercial MOE steel plant.
