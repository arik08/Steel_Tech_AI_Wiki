# Midrex selected for Big River DRI plant

- Title: Midrex Selected for U. S. Steel's First-of-Its-Kind DRI Facility at
  Big River Steel Works
- Publisher: Midrex Technologies, Inc.
- Published: 2026-06-15
- URL: https://www.midrex.com/press-release/midrex-selected-for-u-s-steels-first-of-its-kind-dri-facility-at-big-river-steel-works/
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified equipment and schedule

- Midrex states it was selected to supply a **2.5 million metric tonnes/year
  HDRI/HBI MIDREX plant** at Big River Steel Works.
- The configuration is a **MIDREX Flex** plant. It will hot-charge HDRI to
  adjacent EAFs and can produce HBI for merchant sales and export.
- First production is expected in **2029**.
- The supplier identifies the current process as natural-gas based and capable
  of hydrogen addition. This is not evidence that the initial operation will
  use 100% hydrogen.

## Interpretation boundary

- Capacity and first-production date are supplier design and schedule
  disclosures, not achieved output.
- Midrex's statement that natural-gas MIDREX combined with an electric melter
  can cut CO2 by up to 50% is a generic route claim, not measured performance
  for Big River's DRI-EAF configuration.
