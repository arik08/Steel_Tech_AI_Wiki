# Electra — USD 30 million venture debt for first commercial facility preparation

- Original title: Electra Secures $30M in New Capital to Accelerate Commercial Production
- Publisher: Electra / GlobeNewswire
- Published: 2026-03-10
- URL: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/10/3252582/0/en/Electra-Secures-30M-in-New-Capital-to-Accelerate-Commercial-Production.html
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified statements

- Electra announced a **USD 30 million venture-debt facility from J.P. Morgan**.
- Electra states the capital will be used for **planning and preparation for its first commercial facility**.
- The announcement separately recounts 2025 financing: a USD 186 million Series B round, USD 50 million Breakthrough Energy Catalyst award and USD 8 million Colorado clean-industry tax credit.
- It also repeats a company objective of reaching commercial scale by the end of the 2020s.

## Interpretation boundary

- The USD 30 million facility is debt for commercial-facility planning/preparation; it is not the demonstration plant CAPEX and must not be added to the USD 50 million grant and USD 8 million tax credit as one demonstration budget.
- The announcement does not identify a commercial site, FID, EPC award, capacity commitment or construction start.
- “Capital strength needed to reach commercial scale” is the company's characterization, not proof that financing for a full commercial plant is complete.
