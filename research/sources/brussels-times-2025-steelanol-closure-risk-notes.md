# Steelanol closure-risk report

- Article date: 2025-06-19
- Publisher/byline: The Brussels Times with Belga
- Subject: ArcelorMittal Ghent Steelanol
- Reported status: ArcelorMittal Belgium CEO Frederik Van de Velde said the company was giving itself about one year to decide whether to close Steelanol.
- Reported constraints: unresolved technical issues and an economically difficult regulatory framework even if the plant reached its planned 60,000 t/y ethanol output.
- Regulatory issue reported by the company: the ethanol was not then recognised under EU rules as an advanced biofuel or recycled fuel.
- Boundary: this was a closure consideration and decision window, not a confirmed closure.
- Current-site cross-check collected 2026-07-28: ArcelorMittal Europe still lists Steelanol among the Gent mill's installations and ethanol among its products. This supports “not confirmed closed”, but does not prove continuous operation or full-capacity output.
