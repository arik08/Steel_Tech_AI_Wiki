# JFE carbon-recycling blast furnace operation status

- Title: Policy Engagement — Carbon-Recycling Blast Furnaces
- Publisher: JFE Holdings, Inc.
- URL: https://www.jfe-holdings.co.jp/en/sustainability/environment/climate/steel_industry_efforts/
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified status

- JFE states that its small-scale carbon-recycling test blast furnace was blown
  in and began operation in May 2025.
- The associated climate-action page identifies the pilot as a 150 m³ furnace
  at the Chiba district of East Japan Works and schedules demonstration tests
  through FY2026.
- The program is intended to test high-volume methane and oxygen injection,
  circulation-gas heating and linked operation between the blast furnace and
  the methanation facility.
- JFE's longer-term program target is a greater-than-50% CO2 reduction from the
  blast-furnace steelmaking process using a medium-scale test furnace around
  2030.

## Cross-check

- JFE Group Sustainability Report 2025 independently preserves the 150 m³
  scale, May 2025 demonstration start and FY2026 completion schedule.
- JFE Group Report 2025 likewise says blowing-in was completed in May 2025 and
  operation began.
- These are separate corporate publications from the same issuer, so they
  confirm consistency of JFE's disclosed status but do not constitute
  independent third-party performance verification.

## Interpretation boundary

- “Began operation” applies to the 150 m³ test blast furnace, not a commercial
  blast furnace or a completed integrated CCUS system.
- The greater-than-50% reduction is a program target for a future medium-scale
  test, not a measured result from the 150 m³ furnace.
- The public material does not disclose achieved CO2-reduction rate,
  methanation conversion, hydrogen demand, campaign duration or stable
  integrated-operation hours.
