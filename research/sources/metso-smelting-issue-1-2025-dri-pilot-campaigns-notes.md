# Metso Smelting Issue 1/2025 — Pori DRI pilot commissioned and tested

- Original: https://www.metso.com/campaigns/smelting-newsletter/issue-1-2025/
- Publisher: Metso
- Issue: 1/2025
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified update

- Metso states that it commissioned its own pilot facility for a DRI smelting furnace.
- The company states that it had already conducted two test campaigns with the pilot.
- The same update links the Pori work to Metso's core process design and technology contribution for Fortescue's Christmas Creek Green Metal Project.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- Metso's externally assured 2025 Sustainability statement independently confirms that a Pori DRI Smelting Furnace test campaign successfully completed hot commissioning during 2025.
- The newsletter does not identify the two campaign customers or disclose campaign-level operating data.
- The campaign count is therefore evidence of pilot use, not evidence of continuous commercial availability or industrial-scale performance.
