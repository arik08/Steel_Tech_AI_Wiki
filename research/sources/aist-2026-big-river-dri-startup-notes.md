# AIST cross-check of Big River DRI construction schedule

- Title: U. S. Steel Targets 2029 Start for DRI Plant
- Publisher: Association for Iron & Steel Technology
- Published: 2026-05-04
- URL: https://www.aist.org/u-s-steel-targets-2029-start-for-dri-plant
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Independently reported schedule

- AIST reports that U. S. Steel officials allocated approximately 30 months for
  construction and expected the DRI plant online in the first half of
  **2029**.
- AIST reports that the plant is configured for direct hot charging into Big
  River's furnaces and will consume DR-grade pellets from the Keetac mine.
- The reported project investment is **USD 1.9 billion**.

## Cross-check boundary

- The AIST report independently corroborates the 2029 target later stated by
  Midrex, but it remains a forward schedule rather than an achieved milestone.
- The project should be monitored for permit, groundbreaking, construction and
  commissioning evidence before its status is advanced beyond investment and
  equipment-selection stage.
