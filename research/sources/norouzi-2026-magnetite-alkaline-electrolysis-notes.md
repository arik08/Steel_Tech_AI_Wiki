## Experimental conditions and results

- Mechanically stirred planar-electrode cell.
- 10 wt.% iron-oxide suspension in 50 wt.% NaOH (about 18.3 M) at 110 °C.
- Started potentiostatically at 1.66 V, then transitioned to 1,070 A/m² galvanostatic operation.
- Fine, high-purity synthetic magnetite, d50 about 1.5 μm: 76% Faradaic efficiency.
- Comparable synthetic hematite: 88% Faradaic efficiency.
- Larger synthetic and natural magnetite particles: about 54% Faradaic efficiency.
- The authors associate magnetite’s lower efficiency with stronger hydrogen-evolution activity, and larger particles with increased polarization and accelerated hydrogen evolution.

## Evidence boundary

- The result demonstrates feed mineralogy and particle-size sensitivity in a stirred laboratory cell.
- It is not a continuous ore-preparation/electrolysis/harvesting train, and does not establish stack area, electrode life, electrolyte recycle, impurity management, product qualification, electricity per tonne, or commercial plant throughput.
- These data should not be presented as Volteron or Electra project operating KPIs.
