## Relevant disclosure

The document describes a multistage fine-ore fluidized-bed smelting-reduction
arrangement using separate reactor vessels. Each reactor has a lower reducing-gas
inlet and distributor, a solids inlet and outlet, and a cyclone connected to the
reactor top gas.

The stage cyclone separates entrained fine ore from the gas leaving that reactor.
The separated solids are returned to the lower part of the same reactor, while
the cleaned gas is sent to the next reactor or discharged from the process. The
description gives this routing for the preheating, prereduction, and final
reduction reactors (description corresponding to paragraphs 147–155 in the
Google Patents rendering) and repeats the functional arrangement in claim 1.

## Scope boundary

This is a POSCO/RIST predecessor patent for a three-stage smelting-reduction
configuration. It supports the generic function and routing of stage cyclones in
the FINEX technology lineage; it is not an as-built drawing of the current
four-reactor HyREX demonstration plant. It also does not by itself establish the
exact seal-leg, valve, or pressure-control hardware now selected for HyREX.
