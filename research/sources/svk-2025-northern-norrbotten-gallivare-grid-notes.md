# Svenska kraftnät northern Norrbotten / Gällivare grid notes

- Report: Utvidga transmissionsnätet i norra Norrbottens län
- Publisher: Svenska kraftnät
- Published: 2025
- URL: https://www.svk.se/4aa85f/siteassets/om-oss/rapporter/2025/rapport_regeringsuppdrag-utvidga-transmissionsnatet-i-norra-norrbottens-lan.pdf
- Evidence type: Swedish transmission-system operator report to government.
- Relevant findings:
  - increased transmission demand in Gällivare is driven by LKAB mine expansion, the HYBRIT hydrogen/direct-reduction demonstration plant, and phased large-scale sponge-iron production;
  - decided grid expansion is assessed as sufficient for the first industrial-development stage;
  - current capacity reservation assumes automatic disconnection of industrial loads during some N-1 transmission faults;
  - Svenska kraftnät stated that, based on information then available, LKAB had not yet made the investment decisions required to realize the planned electricity-intensive industrial plants;
  - later stages are not fully covered by reserved capacity and require further reinforcement or nearby generation if all known Gällivare/Kiruna plans proceed.
- Verification boundary:
  - this is an independent infrastructure-planning view, not a final investment decision or construction notice from LKAB;
  - the first-stage capacity assessment does not remove coordination, reliability, or later-stage capacity risks.
