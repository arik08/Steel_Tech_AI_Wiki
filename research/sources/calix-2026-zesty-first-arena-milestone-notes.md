# Calix 2026 — first ZESTY ARENA grant milestone

- Title: Calix completes first ARENA grant milestone for Zesty, triggering $2m cash payment
- Publisher: Calix Limited / ASX announcement
- Published: 2026-03-16
- Official news index: https://calix.global/news/
- Announcement copy: https://investorpa.com/announcement-pdf/20260316/269605.pdf
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Milestone and funding

- Calix reported completion of the first milestone under the ARENA grant
  agreement, triggering the first A$2 million grant payment.
- A further A$42.9 million of the A$44.9 million ARENA grant remained
  outstanding and conditional on matched funding and later milestones through
  commissioning and operation.
- The announcement also recorded a A$3 million Rio Tinto payment received in
  December 2025; more than A$32 million of Rio Tinto cash and in-kind support
  remained outstanding and conditional under the JDA.

## Interpretation boundary

- A milestone payment confirms completion of the contracted early commercial
  and technical deliverables; it is not FID, full financing, EPC award,
  construction start, commissioning or production.
- The later May 2026 ARENA/Calix kick-off report still described the project as
  Pre-FID and listed detailed design, permit, utilities and reliable hydrogen
  supply as remaining prerequisites. The two sources therefore cross-check
  progress without upgrading the project to construction.
