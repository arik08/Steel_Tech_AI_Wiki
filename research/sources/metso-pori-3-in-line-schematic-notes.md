# Metso Pori 3-in-line DRI Smelting Furnace schematic — source notes

- Metso states that it was building a pilot-scale 3-in-line DRI Smelting Furnace at its research center in Pori, Finland.
- Figure 1 is captioned “Schematic presentation of the new 3-in-line pilot furnace in Metso Research Center in Pori, Finland.”
- Metso describes the rectangular pilot as a smaller version of the industrial 6-in-line furnace.
- Approximate furnace dimensions stated in the article are 3 m × 2 m, with a 1,000 kg/h feed rate.
- The stated purposes include Metso development work and customer-feed testing; this is pilot and scale-up evidence, not commercial continuous-operation evidence.
