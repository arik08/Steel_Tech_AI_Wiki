# DOE/NETL Nucor Gallatin dual-loop capture environmental review

- Title: UK IDEA Dual Loop CO2 Capture for Flue Gas with <100 ppm CO2 at
  Nucor Steel Gallatin
- Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy, National Energy Technology Laboratory
- Reviewed: 2024-08-27
- URL: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2025-01/CX-032360.pdf
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified project scope

- DOE/NETL award DE-FE0032601 identifies the University of Kentucky as prime
  recipient and Nucor Steel Gallatin in Ghent, Kentucky as the host site.
- The planned work is to design and install a polishing CO2-capture loop,
  integrate it with the existing underlying capture technology, then test and
  analyze the integrated system.
- The review also includes techno-economic and life-cycle analysis, technology
  gap analysis, environmental health and safety, air-dispersion modeling and
  measurement of relevant emissions.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- The DOE Round 5 selection page separately reports USD 3.0 million DOE funding,
  USD 750,001 non-DOE funding and a USD 3,750,001 total project value
  (SRC-20260725-6D04090A).
- The categorical-exclusion approval confirms the defined installation and
  test scope, but it is not evidence that construction, integrated testing or
  the less-than-100-ppm target has been completed.
- This EAF-flue-gas polishing project is distinct from Nucor's commercial
  Convent DRI carbon-transport-and-storage agreement.
