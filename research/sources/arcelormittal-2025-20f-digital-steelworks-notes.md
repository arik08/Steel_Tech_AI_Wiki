# ArcelorMittal 2025 Form 20-F: digital and AI deployment

- Reporting period: year ended 2025-12-31
- Filing date: 2026-03-06
- ArcelorMittal reports a 2025 initiative named Accelerate Digital Innovation (ADII), with six plants selected across geographies and flat/long product lines for accelerated digital adoption.
- It reports an internally developed predictive-maintenance platform deployed in major plants to monitor critical equipment.
- It reports robotics development focused on safety and operating efficiency.
- It reports a mature AI-based product-development platform used to accelerate development of multiple product families; in the EAF transition, AI was already guiding industrial trials.
- It reports advanced reheating-furnace energy models industrialised across 23 furnaces at 19 plants; upgraded online integration was implemented at 14 furnaces, with up to 7% higher output in some cases.
- Boundary: the filing does not name all six ADII plants, disclose model accuracy, or provide plant-by-plant causal validation for the “up to 7%” output result. These are company-reported deployment claims, not independently audited digital-performance benchmarks.
