# PURESCRAP 12th Steering Committee meeting

- Title: 12th Steering Committee Meeting
- Publisher: PURESCRAP consortium
- Published: 2026-02-25
- URL: https://purescrap.eu/?p=1062
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified progress statement

- At the first PURESCRAP steering committee meeting of 2026, the partners said
  that results from recent test campaigns were being evaluated.
- The consortium also said that preparations for the next phase of experiments
  were already underway.
- The notice announced that SSSA would present the project and its latest
  results at the European Electric Steelmaking Conference in Milan.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- The European Commission CORDIS results record, updated 2026-05-03, separately
  lists intermediate KPI-monitoring and melting/quality-steel reports plus
  functional heavy- and shredded-scrap demonstrator configurations
  (SRC-20260728-E41E2371).
- Together, the two sources support an interpretation of ongoing experimental
  validation in early 2026. They do not establish that impurity-removal,
  post-consumer-scrap-share, steel-quality or CO2 targets had been achieved.
- The consortium website still displays the historical June 2026 end date,
  whereas the newer CORDIS grant record reports 2027-03-31. The CORDIS date is
  used for the current contractual schedule.
