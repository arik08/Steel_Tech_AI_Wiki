# Mortadi et al. 2026 H2-DRI shaft-system model notes

- Title: System analysis of direct reduced iron production with elevated levels of hydrogen in the shaft furnace
- Authors: Marwa Mortadi; Carl Haikarainen; Guanwei Zhou; Henrik Saxén
- Venue: Fuel 420 (2026), article 138979
- DOI: 10.1016/j.fuel.2026.138979
- Published online: 2026-03-03
- Evidence type: peer-reviewed, open-access journal article
- Scope: Aspen Plus system model of an industrial-scale shaft furnace plus gas-handling units, with green hydrogen and natural gas in the fresh feed.
- Main modeled result: increasing hydrogen in the fresh feed reduced modeled CO2 emissions by about two thirds while modeled DRI metallization remained above 90%.
- Trade-off: top-gas recycle raised CO in the reducing gas and the modeled metallization rate, but could reduce methane conversion in the reformer.
- Pressure trade-off: higher process-gas pressure improved modeled metallization and lowered pressure drop, but lowered methane conversion.
- Verification boundary: these are simulation outputs and operating-state guidance, not a report of commercial 100% hydrogen DRI plant operation, equipment guarantees, or measured HYBRIT/HyREX/HYFOR performance.
