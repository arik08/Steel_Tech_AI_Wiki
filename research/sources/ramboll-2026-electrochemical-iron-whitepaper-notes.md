# Ramboll 2026 — electrochemical iron-production comparison

- Title: Forging a cleaner future — Advances and business models in electrochemical iron production
- Publisher: Ramboll
- URL: https://c.ramboll.com/hubfs/Low-Carbon-Steel/Electrochemical-Whitepaper.pdf
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Type: engineering-consultancy white paper; not identified as peer reviewed

## Cross-route observations

- The paper compares molten oxide electrolysis with aqueous electrowinning,
  covering Boston Metal, Electra, ULCOWIN/SIDERWIN and historic iron
  electrowinning.
- It describes Electra's separation of acidic ore dissolution, acid regeneration
  and plating into coupled subsystems, and records pilot production of about
  2 m2, 60 kg cathode sheets at around 60°C.
- It describes persistent SIDERWIN scale-up issues, including dendrite-driven
  short circuits and cell modifications, while also recording production of
  larger high-purity iron plates.

## Independent assessment and boundary

- Ramboll concludes that both MOE and aqueous electrowinning may use low-grade
  resources and offer smaller modular facilities, but that energy and cost
  efficiency remain unproven at large scale.
- The white paper is an independent engineering synthesis, not an audited plant
  test. Many project-specific values are traced to company, consortium and media
  sources.
- Funding raised, plate size, high purity and pilot operation must not be treated
  as proof of commercial throughput, availability, electrode life or
  competitive delivered cost.
- One SIDERWIN specific-energy figure in the document appears dimensionally
  questionable in the displayed units and is not used as a Wiki claim without
  returning to the primary project report.
