# EC INCITE Volteron current maturity cross-check

- Source: European Commission, Innovation Centre for Industrial Transformation and Emissions (INCITE), “Low temperature electrolysis of iron ore in an aqueous alkaline solution - Volteron™”
- URL: https://innovation-centre-for-industrial-transformation.ec.europa.eu/innovative-techniques/low-temperature-electrolysis-iron-ore-aqueous-alkaline-solution-volterontm
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Confirmed points

- INCITE describes the 2025 Volteron pilot as a 1 m² cell with industrial-size cathodes and anodes.
- The page reports production of about 140 kg of metallic iron in four plates, using two cathodes and three anodes, and says the pilot had been running since April 2025.
- It describes 2023–2025 as the industrial-plant engineering phase with John Cockerill and says the engineering and full CAPEX study was to be completed by the end of 2025.
- Deployment of the first scale-up plant remains conditional on pilot results, framework conditions, and funding.

## Interpretation boundary

- The body text says the pilot was expected to validate TRL 7 by the end of 2025, while the structured “Basic information” field labels achieved TRL as 7. This internal presentation difference is preserved rather than converted into a stronger maturity claim.
- Neither the pilot result nor engineering/CAPEX study demonstrates final investment decision, construction, continuous industrial operation, or commercial output.
- Energy and emissions figures on the page are estimates or targets and are not treated as independently measured full-scale performance.
