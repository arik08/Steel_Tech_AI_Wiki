## Current pilot status

- A third pilot-scale campaign is planned to start later in 2026 at the Calix Technology Centre, Bacchus Marsh.
- Planned upgrades are intended to:
  - enable semi-continuous operation and larger DRI sample volumes for downstream customer testing;
  - improve ultrafines handling and product yield;
  - test natural gas and reformed natural gas as transitional alternative reductants.
- These are planned campaign capabilities and objectives, not completed results.

## Demonstration-project status and funding boundary

- Calix still describes the 30,000 t/y Kwinana demonstration as progressing “towards a Final Investment Decision”.
- Calix states that it has received:
  - the first ARENA grant payment of A$2 million;
  - the first Rio Tinto cash contribution of A$3 million.
- The overall ARENA award remains “up to A$44.9 million” subject to matched funding.
- Rio Tinto support remains “over A$35 million” in cash and in-kind support, subject to milestones.
- The initial payments are not evidence that FID, EPC, construction, commissioning, or 30,000 t/y output has occurred.

## Cross-check

- The ARENA May 2026 kick-off report independently described completion of detailed engineering, works-permit transfer/amendment, public comment and hydrogen supply as FID prerequisites.
- The July Calix update is consistent with the project remaining pre-FID and adds planned third-pilot-campaign scope; it does not supersede those prerequisites.
