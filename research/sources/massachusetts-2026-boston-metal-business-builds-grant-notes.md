# Massachusetts 2026 — Boston Metal Business Builds grant

- Title: Healey-Driscoll Administration Awards $4.45 Million to Expand
  Advanced Manufacturing, Create Jobs Across Massachusetts
- Publisher: Massachusetts Executive Office of Economic Development
- Published: 2026-02-11
- URL: https://www.mass.gov/news/healey-driscoll-administration-awards-445-million-to-expand-advanced-manufacturing-create-jobs-across-massachusetts
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Official award

- Massachusetts announced a USD 950,308 Business Builds Capital Grant award
  to Boston Metal in Woburn.
- The release describes Boston Metal's platform as serving both critical-metal
  recovery and lower-carbon steel production.
- The release's 90 projected jobs and more than USD 52 million in private
  investment are aggregate figures for Boston Metal and two other awardees;
  they are not Boston Metal-only values.

## Timing and interpretation boundary

- The award was announced on 2026-02-11, before the 2026-02-24 reporting of
  Boston Metal's WARN notice and planned Woburn workforce reduction.
- A grant award is not evidence that the Woburn steel programme subsequently
  maintained staffing, avoided restructuring, achieved continuous operation or
  restored an earlier commercialization schedule.
