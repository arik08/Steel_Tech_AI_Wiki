# John Cockerill Volteron official product page

- Page collected: 2026-07-28
- Publisher: John Cockerill
- The page identifies Volteron as low-temperature iron electrowinning co-developed with ArcelorMittal.
- It describes direct production of high-purity iron plates from iron ore using renewable electricity, followed by melting/steelmaking.
- The page displays an official photograph captioned “Siderwin pilot plant” and an official Volteron route graphic.
- It lists an industrial-scale target of 40,000–80,000 t/y by 2027 and later scaling to 1 Mt/y or more.
- Boundary: the target is a supplier roadmap, not evidence that the industrial plant has reached FID, construction, or operation. ArcelorMittal's 2025 Form 20-F separately confirms successful 1:1 vertical-cell operation and completion of industrial engineering design.
