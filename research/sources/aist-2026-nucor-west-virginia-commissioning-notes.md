# AIST cross-check: Nucor West Virginia commissioning

- Publication date: 2026-06-16
- Publisher: Association for Iron & Steel Technology
- AIST reported that Nucor had begun commissioning components of the new West Virginia sheet mill in May 2026.
- Nucor president and COO Steve Laxton said commissioning would continue in sequence through the rest of 2026.
- The reported design capacity is 3 million tons/year.
- AIST reported a year-end 2026 start-up direction and described 2027 as the ramp-up year, with approximately 50% utilisation possible in 2027 if market conditions support it.
- Cross-check boundary: Nucor's Q1 presentation states “production expected 2027”; therefore a 2026 “start-up” should be read as commissioning/start-up activities, not established commercial production.
