# You et al. 2026 generalized grain-scale H-DRI/HPSR model — source note

- Publisher page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0009250926006950
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ces.2026.123983
- Title: *A generalized Grain-Scale model for the Non-Plasma and
  Plasma-Assisted hydrogen direct reduction of iron ore*
- Authors: Rui You; Hariswaran Sitaraman; Sridhar Seetharaman; Laxminarayan L. Raja
- Journal: Chemical Engineering Science 331, article 123983
- Academic status: peer-reviewed journal article, independently confirmed by
  the National Laboratory of the Rockies publication record.

## Model contribution

- The authors introduce a Transient Reactive Grain Model (TRGM) for a single
  micron-scale iron-oxide grain.
- The model couples gas-species transport, material conversion kinetics,
  nanopore structural evolution, and adsorption/desorption surface kinetics.
- It explicitly distinguishes atomic hydrogen from molecular hydrogen, allowing
  plasma-assisted and non-plasma reduction to be represented in the same
  framework.
- The authors report agreement with experimental data for molecular-hydrogen
  reduction of Fe2O3 and plasma-hydrogen reduction of Fe3O4.
- The calculations show diffuse reaction zones and enhanced hydrogen diffusion
  near the grain exterior as porosity evolves, challenging sharp-front,
  quasi-steady and fixed-porosity assumptions.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- ScienceDirect provides the technical abstract and DOI; the National
  Laboratory of the Rockies lists the item as a peer-reviewed journal article.
- The paper models a single micron-scale grain and uses selected experimental
  datasets for validation. It does not validate a complete pellet, reactor,
  continuous pilot, energy consumption per tonne, refractory life, product
  quality, or commercial throughput.
- The publication is useful for multi-scale model boundary conditions, not as
  evidence that a flash or plasma ironmaking plant has achieved scale-up.
