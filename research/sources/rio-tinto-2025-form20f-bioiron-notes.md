# Rio Tinto 2025 Form 20-F — BioIron construction pause

- Title: Annual Report on Form 20-F 2025
- Issuer: Rio Tinto plc / Rio Tinto Limited
- Filed/published: 2026-02-19
- SEC HTML: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/863064/000162828026009531/rio-20251231.htm
- Rio Tinto annual-report page: https://www.riotinto.com/en/invest/reports/annual-report
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified formal-report status

- Rio Tinto's filed annual report says that BioIron pilot-plant work and the
  associated commissioning target were paused because of technical and design
  challenges associated with early-stage innovation.
- It says R&D continues with the University of Nottingham and Metso.
- The report identifies progress in understanding high-temperature material
  behaviour, while stating that the current furnace design needs more
  development to minimise technical risk and optimise performance.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- This formal annual-report disclosure confirms the status first announced by
  Rio Tinto on 2025-11-17 and independently reported by ABC News.
- “Paused” and continuing R&D do not support a conclusion that the Western
  Australia 1 t/h pilot remains on its previous commissioning schedule.
- The filing does not provide a new construction restart date, revised furnace
  design, EPC milestone, cost-to-complete or achieved 1 t/h run.
