- Title: Thermodynamic-kinetic modeling of hematite reduction in a

## Reported model and validation envelope

- The authors built a dynamic model coupling plasma-equilibrium boundary
  conditions, metal/slag mass balances and mass-transfer-limited reduction
  kinetics.
- It predicts the hematite → magnetite → wüstite → iron sequence and was
  compared with laboratory metal/slag masses, XRF and XRD observations.
- The experimental envelope was a laboratory arc-melting furnace under
  90 vol% Ar–10 vol% H2 at 1 atm and 5 L/min, 200 A, a 10 mm arc length and
  12.5 minute reduction cycles.

## Interpretation boundary

- Agreement inside the laboratory validation envelope supports mechanistic
  interpretation, but does not validate pilot-scale residence time, gas
  recycle, electrode and refractory life, continuous feeding/tapping or
  industrial energy intensity.
- The paper reports that underlying data are available on request; therefore
  independent numerical reproduction was not performed during this run.
