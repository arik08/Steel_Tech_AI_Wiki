# Mohanta et al. 2026 — spatially resolved HPSR diagnostics preprint

- Title: Spatiotemporal Dynamics of Hydrogen Plasma Smelting Reduction of iron ore: A Multi-Species Diagnostic Approach
- Repository: arXiv
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06840
- Authors: Ram Krushna Mohanta; Kai Lopez; Sai Vishnu Korsipati; Hariswaran Sitaraman; Laxminarayan Raja; Noemi Leick; Seetharaman Sridhar
- Submitted: 2026-07-07
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Review status: preprint; peer review not confirmed

## Diagnostic result

- The authors combined spatially resolved multi-species optical emission
  spectroscopy, infrared thermography and an LTE thermal-plasma model.
- They report a strongly stratified discharge: an argon-defined core above
  10,000 K, a hydrogen-Balmer envelope around 7,000–10,000 K, an Fe I-traced
  interfacial layer around 3,000–4,000 K, and a melt surface around
  1,900–2,300 K.
- Across that gradient, positive hydrogen ions are reported to recombine before
  reaching the oxide surface; the inferred reductant flux at the interface is
  predominantly neutral atomic hydrogen and vibrationally excited H2.

## Interpretation boundary

- The spatial diagnostics independently reinforce the neutral-species
  mechanism reported by the group's peer-reviewed interface-model paper, but
  this item remains a preprint and should not carry the same evidentiary weight.
- The measured and modeled temperature fields are reactor- and
  configuration-specific. They are not a universal HPSR temperature profile
  and do not prove pilot throughput, energy intensity, refractory life,
  hydrogen utilization or commercial availability.
