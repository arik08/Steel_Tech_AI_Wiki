- 발행자: Amazon Web Services / About Amazon

- 원문: https://press.aboutamazon.com/aws/2026/6/arcelormittal-announces-strategic-collaboration-with-aws-to-drive-industrial-automation-and-lower-carbon-construction-globally

- AWS도 ArcelorMittal과의 cloud, AI, edge 협력과 OT/IT 수렴 방향을 동일하게
  발표했다.

  열거한다.
