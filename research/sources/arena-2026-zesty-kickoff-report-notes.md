## Verified source notes

The report states that Calix is in the pre-FID task phase. Passing FID requires
completion of detailed engineering, finalisation of works permits, public-comment
stakeholder work, utilities, and a reliable hydrogen supply option.

The selected site is in the Kwinana-Rockingham Strategic Industrial Area in
Western Australia. The project uses the site previously developed for Rio
Tinto's BioIron project. Initial meetings with the Western Australian Department
of Water and Environmental Regulation were completed, but amendments to the
works approval still need to proceed through public comment.

The Rio Tinto joint development agreement provides cash and in-kind support,
ore for commissioning and tolling, and support for deployment and marketing.
Calix was still seeking additional institutional and strategic co-investment
when the report was prepared.

Calix reports more than 130 pilot runs with multiple ores and identifies the
technology as TRL 5, with the demonstration plant intended to advance it to TRL
7. The planned plant is one commercial-scale reactor unit whose modular
replication is intended to support industrial expansion. These are project and
developer classifications, not independent proof of commercial operation.

The report identifies material schedule and cost risks:

- renewable hydrogen in Kwinana-Rockingham is currently expensive and not
  readily accessible;
- natural-gas-derived hydrogen is also being considered for demonstration
  operations;
- Pilbara ultra-fines create production and handling challenges that require
  de-risking;
- Western Australian labour, services, utilities, permitting, logistics, and
  large-load grid-connection requirements may increase costs or extend the
  schedule;
- EPC delivery may take longer than the eastern-Australia reference schedule;
- later milestones may change as the site-specific engineering studies finish.

The high-level schedule graphic on page 5 shows pre-FID tasks in 2026 followed
by procurement, detailed design and construction, then commissioning,
operations, product/process validation, knowledge sharing and commercial-scale
pre-FEED/FEED extending into the 2031-2032 range. The report does not present
these later bars as completed milestones, and explicitly warns that schedule
refinement and risk review may affect them.
