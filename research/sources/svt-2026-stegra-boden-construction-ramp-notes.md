# SVT — Stegra Boden construction ramp remains below full pace

- Original: https://www.svt.se/nyheter/lokalt/norrbotten/stegra-trappar-upp-i-boden-men-langt-ifran-full-fart
- Publisher: SVT Nyheter Norrbotten
- Published: 2026-06-11
- Updated: 2026-06-11
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Independently reported physical progress

- SVT reported that activity at the Boden construction site remained well below the most intensive 2025 level after contractors had left and work slowed during financing uncertainty.
- Stegra's COO said the company intended to ramp site staffing toward 4,000–5,000 people during summer 2026.
- The visible DRI tower had reached about 120 m, with a planned final height of 145 m.
- The COO did not provide a new, more precise production-start forecast. SVT noted the earlier company target of early 2027.

## Cross-check and interpretation boundary

- Stegra's 2026-03-27 official update reported the DRI tower passing 100 m, consistent with SVT's later 120 m observation.
- Stegra's 2026-06-24 financing close release confirmed that construction had been slower during financing and that the project schedule remained under review.
- The physical tower milestone and staffing plan demonstrate ongoing construction, but not completion, commissioning or adherence to the earlier early-2027 production target.
