# ExxonMobil 2030 plan - conditional Nucor CCS start-up target

- Title: ExxonMobil raises its 2030 Plan
- Publisher: Exxon Mobil Corporation
- Published: 2025-12-09
- URL: https://corporate.exxonmobil.com/news/news-releases/2025/1209-exxonmobil-raises-2030-plan-transformation
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified statement

- ExxonMobil stated that additional CCS projects with partners including
  **Nucor**, Linde and New Generation Gas Gathering were expected to start up
  in **2026**.

## Conditions and interpretation boundary

- The release explicitly conditions the schedule on additional ExxonMobil
  investment, supportive government policy and receipt of government permits.
- The statement is a forward-looking portfolio target, not evidence that the
  Nucor Convent capture, transport or storage chain entered operation.
- ExxonMobil's May 2026 climate report continued to describe Nucor as an
  announced project under contract and did not include it among the two projects
  then transporting and storing CO2.
