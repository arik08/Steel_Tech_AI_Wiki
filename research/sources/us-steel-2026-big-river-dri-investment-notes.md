# U. S. Steel Big River DRI investment and site integration

- Title: U. S. Steel Announces First-of-Its-Kind in the United States DRI
  Facility at Big River Steel Works
- Publisher: United States Steel Corporation
- Published: 2026-04-29
- URL: https://www.ussteel.com/media/newsroom/-/blogs/u-s-steel-announces-first-of-its-kind-in-the-united-states-dri-facility-at-big-river-steel-works
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified scope

- U. S. Steel announced **USD 1.9 billion** to build a DRI facility at Big
  River Steel Works in Osceola, Arkansas.
- The project connects Keetac DR-grade pellet production with on-site DRI and
  the four EAFs at Big River Steel Works.
- U. S. Steel reports that the Big River 2 expansion is in full production.
- The company expects approximately 200 permanent employee roles, 35 embedded
  contractor roles and about 2,000 construction jobs at peak.

## Interpretation boundary

- The release does not itself provide DRI capacity or the first-production
  date.
- "First of its kind" is the company's characterization; the precise boundary
  is an integrated U.S. DRI-EAF facility using a domestic DR-grade pellet
  supply chain.
