- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Cross-check result

- The independent technology publication reports that Boston Metal operated its largest steelmaking reactor and produced more than one tonne of metal.
- This is directionally consistent with Boston Metal's 2025-03-12 announcement that a multi-inert-anode industrial MOE cell was commissioned and produced “tonnage steel.”
- The report supports that an industrial-scale cell operated and produced a tonne-class batch/output event. It does not provide audited continuous throughput, energy consumption, current efficiency, anode life, campaign duration or commercial plant economics.
- A GlobeNewswire version repeats Boston Metal's own release and is not treated as independent corroboration.

## Interpretation boundary

- “Industrial cell” is the company's scale designation for the Woburn unit.
- The separate MOE Steel demonstration plant remains a future step; the Woburn cell milestone must not be rewritten as completion of a commercial steel plant.
