# MIDREX H2 — 2017 official process concept and current cross-check

- Title: MIDREX H2: Ultimate Low CO2 Ironmaking and its place in the new Hydrogen Economy
- Publisher: Midrex Technologies, Inc.
- Authors: Jayson Ripke and John Kopfle
- Page date: September 2017
- URL: https://www.midrex.com/tech-article/midrex-h2-ultimate-low-co2-ironmaking-and-its-place-in-the-new-hydrogen-economy/
- Collected: 2026-07-28

## Verified technical content

- Figure 4 is the equipment supplier's MIDREX H2 process flowsheet. It shows a shaft furnace, top-gas scrubber, process-gas compressor, hydrogen make-up, reduction-gas heater and DRI/HBI product routes.
- The article describes hydrogen generated outside the DR process, removal of the conventional reformer, heating and recycle of the reducing gas, and condensation of product water in the top-gas scrubber.
- The 2017 concept still includes a limited natural-gas addition for temperature control and DRI carburisation. It therefore must not be presented as a universal drawing of every current nominally 100% H2 MIDREX project.

## Current cross-check

- Midrex's current MIDREX H2 product page says greenfield configurations are designed for 100% hydrogen or majority-hydrogen operation with minimal natural gas, use an electric heater for recirculating hydrogen, and are based on the proven shaft-furnace and process-gas platform:
  https://www.midrex.com/midrex-process/midrex-h2/
- The old Figure 4 and the current product description agree on the core equipment boundary: shaft furnace, recycle-gas cleaning/compression, external hydrogen supply and gas heating.
- They differ in commercial framing and optional hydrocarbon use. The figure is therefore retained as an official functional flowsheet, not as a current project as-built drawing or a guarantee of zero natural-gas consumption.
