# Tokushige and Haarberg 2026 — alkaline suspension electrolysis

- Title: Production of Iron from Iron Oxide Powder by Suspension Electrolysis
  in Alkaline Electrolyte
- Journal: The Journal of Physical Chemistry C, 130(8), 2988–2996
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcc.5c08453
- Authors: Manabu Tokushige; Geir Martin Haarberg
- Published online: 2026-02-12
- Collected: 2026-07-28
- Peer review: journal research article

## Experimental findings

- Iron was electrodeposited from Fe2O3 or Fe3O4 powder suspensions in
  25 mol/kg NaOH–H2O or KOH–H2O electrolyte at 110 °C.
- Above 300 mA/cm², faradaic efficiency decreased as hydrogen evolution
  increased; gas-bubble coverage was associated with dendritic iron growth.
- Comparing hematite and magnetite showed that oxide conductivity and particle
  aggregation affect polarization loss and faradaic efficiency.

## Interpretation boundary

- The reported steady-state cell voltage is an apparent operating voltage that
  combines polarization, ohmic loss and side reactions. Energy descriptors
  derived from it are not a demonstrated plant energy intensity.
- The result is an electrochemical materials experiment, not a continuous
  ore-to-saleable-iron pilot demonstration. Electrolyte circulation, solids
  handling, deposit recovery, impurity control, electrode life and downstream
  melting at industrial scale remain outside the reported evidence.
