# Mohanta et al. 2026 HPSR multi-species diagnostics — source note

- Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06840
- Title: *Spatiotemporal Dynamics of Hydrogen Plasma Smelting Reduction of iron ore: A Multi-Species Diagnostic Approach*
- Authors: Ram Krushna Mohanta; Kai Lopez; Sai Vishnu Korsipati; Hariswaran Sitaraman; Laxminarayan Raja; Noemi Leick; Seetharaman Sridhar
- Submitted: 2026-07-07
- Academic status: arXiv preprint; peer review is not established by the record.
- arXiv DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2607.06840

## Reported diagnostic result

- The study combines spatially and temporally resolved multi-species optical
  emission spectroscopy, melt-surface infrared thermography, and an LTE thermal
  plasma model.
- It reports a stratified, non-isothermal transferred arc:
  - argon-defined core above 10,000 K;
  - partially recombined hydrogen Balmer envelope at 7,000–10,000 K;
  - Fe I-traced interfacial boundary layer at 3,000–4,000 K;
  - melt surface at approximately 1,900–2,300 K.
- The authors infer that positive hydrogen ions recombine before reaching the
  oxide surface, leaving atomic hydrogen and vibrationally excited molecular
  hydrogen as the dominant reductant flux at the interface.

## Interpretation boundary and cross-check

- These measurements refine kinetic-model boundary conditions and challenge a
  spatially uniform arc assumption.
- The paper does not report industrial throughput, metallization rate, energy
  consumption per tonne, refractory life, continuous campaign duration, or
  commercial-scale validation.
- CORDIS H2PlasmaRed periodic reporting independently identifies optical
  emission diagnostics and dynamic modeling as project work, but it does not
  independently reproduce the temperatures or species distribution reported
  in this preprint.
