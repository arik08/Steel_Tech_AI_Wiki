# NIST Digital Twins for Advanced Manufacturing

NIST's advanced-manufacturing digital-twin program aims to provide measurement
science and open standards so manufacturers can define, measure, analyze and
control complex systems with trustworthy digital twins.

The program emphasizes reference architecture, interoperability and lifecycle
data integration. It calls for verification, validation and uncertainty
quantification (VVUQ) of data, models and twin results. Testbeds and reference
implementations are used to assess accuracy, trustworthiness and
interoperability.

The program also develops digital-thread implementations that connect design,
production and maintenance data. The ISO 23247 manufacturing digital-twin
framework was published in 2021. A visual resemblance or dashboard alone is
therefore not enough to establish a credible operational twin; synchronization,
fit-for-purpose validation, uncertainty and traceable data flow are required.
