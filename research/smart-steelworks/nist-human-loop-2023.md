# NIST Human-in-the-Loop Smart Manufacturing Architecture

NIST authors describe smart manufacturing as seeking near-optimal, sustainable
and resilient solutions. Although some traditional processes are software
controlled, many manufacturing processes still require human involvement.

The proposed digital-twin and human-in-the-loop architecture connects physical,
cyber and human spaces. AI and human cognition jointly support real-time
decisions. This distinction is important for steelmaking: advisory prediction,
operator-approved control and autonomous closed-loop control are different
deployment stages, and human authority should be explicit rather than inferred
from the word automation.
