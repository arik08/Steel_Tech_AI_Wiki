# POSCO One-Touch Converter Operation Automation

POSCO reported that on 2024-10-24 its president remotely initiated Gwangyang
Works No. 2 converter from Seoul and produced top-grade automotive steel.
Development began in 2018. The integrated system reduced 25 manual converter
operations to a one-touch sequence.

IoT cameras and vision measurement automate tapping, slag separation and
coating. A model trained with three years of operations data predicts molten
steel temperature and composition. POSCO reports that accuracy improved from
94% to 97%, error was reduced, and estimated annual economic benefit is
approximately KRW 33.8 billion.

The digital twin synchronizes a virtual converter with the physical process for
integrated monitoring and remote control. The system automates operations, but
operators retain monitoring and management responsibility. POSCO announced a
roadmap to extend the technology from Gwangyang to Pohang and Indonesia; that
roadmap is not evidence that those sites had completed deployment.
