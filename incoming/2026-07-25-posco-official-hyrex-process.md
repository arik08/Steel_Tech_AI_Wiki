# POSCO official HyREX process

POSCO describes HyREX as a hydrogen-reduction ironmaking route that converts
fine iron ore into direct reduced iron using hydrogen and then melts the DRI in
an electric smelting furnace to produce hot metal.

The official process illustration shows fine ore passing through four
sequential fluidized-bed reactors. Clean hydrogen enters the reduction train,
the resulting DRI is transferred to an electric smelting furnace, and the hot
metal then proceeds to a converter and continuous casting. Carbon-free
electricity supplies the electric smelting furnace and downstream production
route.

POSCO explains that the multiple fluidized-bed reactors allow temperature
control by reactor because hydrogen reduction is endothermic. The route is
based on POSCO's commercial FINEX fluidized-bed experience and is intended to
use fine ore without pelletizing.
