# European Commission state-aid decision for thyssenkrupp Steel Duisburg

The European Commission announced on 2023-07-20 that it had approved German
support for thyssenkrupp Steel Europe's Duisburg direct-reduction transition.

The approved measures comprise a direct grant of up to EUR 550 million for
construction and installation of a direct-reduction plant and two melters, plus
a conditional payment mechanism of up to EUR 1.45 billion to support the
accelerated uptake of renewable hydrogen during the first ten operating years.

The Commission described an initial natural-gas operating phase followed by
renewable-hydrogen substitution, and an original 2026 start target for
2.3 million tonnes per year of lower-carbon hot metal. Those 2023 timing and
capacity statements are historical approval assumptions and must not replace
the project's later company schedule of commissioning beginning in 2027 and
2.5 million tonnes per year of DRI.
