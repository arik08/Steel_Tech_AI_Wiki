# Low-Temperature Electrowinning of Iron from Mixed Hematite-Magnetite Alkaline Suspensions

Verified sources:

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/acd085
- EU CORDIS SIDERWIN publication record:
  https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/768788/results

Authors: Clara Boehme, Maksim Starykevich, Gabriel Constantinescu,
Andrei V. Kovalevsky, Daniela V. Lopes.

Published in Journal of The Electrochemical Society, volume 170, number 5,
2023. The study examines low-temperature electrowinning from alkaline
suspensions containing mixed hematite and magnetite rather than a single
reagent oxide.

The source is relevant to feed-mineral variability in aqueous electrowinning.
It demonstrates research-scale electrochemical treatment of mixed oxides.

Evidence boundary: this paper does not establish tolerance to every commercial
low-grade ore or the performance of a proprietary industrial-scale process.
