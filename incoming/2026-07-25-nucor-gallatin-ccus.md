# Nucor and University of Kentucky carbon-capture pilot at Gallatin

Nucor announced on 2022-04-22 that it was partnering with the University of
Kentucky Research Foundation to test a carbon dioxide capture system at Nucor
Steel Gallatin. The work applies carbon capture to the relatively low-CO2 flue
gas of electric arc furnace steelmaking and was supported by the U.S.
Department of Energy and National Energy Technology Laboratory.

The announcement states that construction and testing of a heat-integrated
capture process had been completed and describes the program as a pilot to
evaluate cost, effectiveness, and replication at other EAF mills. More than 50
industry and university specialists were participating. The announcement does
not report commercial capture capacity, permanent-storage volume, or
continuous commercial operation.
