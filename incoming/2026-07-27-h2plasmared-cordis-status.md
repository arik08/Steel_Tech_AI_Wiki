# H2PlasmaRed CORDIS periodic status

The European Commission CORDIS page records H2PlasmaRed as a Horizon Europe
project running from 2024-01-01 to 2027-12-31, with total cost
EUR 6,374,937.50 and EU contribution EUR 5,997,965.

The project's stated objective is to raise hydrogen plasma smelting reduction
from TRL 5 to TRL 7. Planned demonstrations comprise an integrated
hundred-kilogram-scale HPSR pilot reactor and retrofit of an existing
five-tonne pilot-scale DC electric arc furnace. These are project objectives,
not achieved industrial scale.

The first periodic report, covering work through 2025-06-30, says retrofit and
method-development work had begun. It expected pilot-HPSR trials through the
end of 2025 and completion of the five-tonne DC-EAF demonstration campaign in
2026. Supporting work includes optical-emission sensing, dynamic modelling,
laboratory testing of ores and steelmaking side streams, and initial LCA and
techno-economic assessment.
