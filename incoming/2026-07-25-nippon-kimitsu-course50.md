# Nippon Steel Kimitsu No. 2 blast furnace hydrogen-reduction demonstration

Nippon Steel announced on 2023-02-09 that it would install a hydrogen-rich gas
injection demonstration facility at the No. 2 blast furnace in the Kimitsu
Area of East Nippon Works. The operating blast furnace has a volume of 4,500
cubic metres, around 400 times the 12 cubic metre test furnace used for earlier
COURSE50 development.

The company originally targeted January 2026 to launch the large-scale
demonstration using hydrogen generated in the works. The project is part of a
NEDO Green Innovation Fund consortium with Nippon Steel, JFE Steel, Kobe
Steel, and the Japan Research and Development Center for Metals.

In parallel, the Super COURSE50 test-furnace route uses externally sourced,
heated hydrogen and targets technology capable of reducing blast-furnace CO2
emissions by 50 percent or more. The target is a development objective and
must not be reported as achieved commercial performance.
