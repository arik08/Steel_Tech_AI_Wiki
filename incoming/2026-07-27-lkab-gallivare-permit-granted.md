# LKAB Gällivare environmental permit granted

On 2026-06-15 LKAB announced that the Land and Environmental Court had granted
an environmental permit for continued and expanded operations in Malmberget.
The permit scope includes establishment of a demonstration plant for
fossil-free sponge iron production as well as an apatite processing plant.

LKAB said it still needed to review the ruling and assess how to proceed. The
permit removes a major legal prerequisite but does not by itself constitute a
final investment decision, construction completion, commissioning, or a
revised operating date.
