Source evidence excerpt collected 2026-07-25.

The UNIDO/Agora Industry report "Low-carbon technologies for the global steel
transformation" assesses molten oxide electrolysis as an emerging all-electric
steel route. Its values are scenario assumptions assembled from multiple
sources, not operating data from a commercial MOE steel plant.

The report gives a total energy-demand range of 12.4-14.8 GJ per tonne of crude
steel, all as electricity, and says dedicated electricity infrastructure and
possibly storage may be required. Its modeled 2050 production cost is
USD 582-766 per tonne of crude steel, compared with a 2022 BF-BOF reference of
USD 472-499 per tonne. The modeled MOE range assumes zero direct process CO2.

The report classifies MOE at pilot-plant stage (TRL 3-4) and projects readiness
in 2035-2040. This assessment predates or does not incorporate all details of
Boston Metal's March 2025 multi-anode industrial-cell announcement, so it should
be treated as an external scenario benchmark rather than a current official TRL
determination.
