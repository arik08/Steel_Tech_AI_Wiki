Source evidence excerpt collected 2026-07-25.

Boston Metal announced on 12 March 2025 that it had commissioned a
multi-inert-anode molten oxide electrolysis cell for clean steel production at
its Woburn, Massachusetts facility. The company described the unit as an
industrial cell and stated that tonnage steel was flowing from it.

The release reports that the cell was operational and had produced pure molten
iron. Boston Metal presents the result as evidence that its proprietary inert
anode can operate in the high-temperature and corrosive environment of iron ore
electrolysis and that the multi-anode concept can be scaled. The release does
not disclose continuous campaign duration, current efficiency, specific
electricity consumption, anode wear rate, annualized capacity, product chemistry,
or an independent performance verification.
