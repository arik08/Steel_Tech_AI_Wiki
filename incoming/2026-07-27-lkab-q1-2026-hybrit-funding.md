# LKAB Q1 2026 report — HYBRIT Innovation Fund withdrawal

LKAB's first-quarter 2026 interim report states that, after the reporting
period, the company and CINEA agreed to withdraw the EUR 113 million
Innovation Fund support previously granted for the planned HYBRIT technology
demonstration plant in Gällivare.

LKAB attributed the decision to an updated timeline and delays, particularly
the environmental permitting process, which meant that time-bound grant
milestones could not be met. The report states that none of the granted amount
had been disbursed and that LKAB planned to prepare a new EU application
aligned with updated conditions and timing.

The withdrawal is a schedule-and-funding risk signal. It is not a project
cancellation and should not be represented as repayment of money already
received.
