# Hydrogen Uses in Ironmaking — evidence excerpt

Source: https://www.energy.gov/sites/prod/files/2018/08/f54/fcto-h2-scale-kickoff-2018-8-chevrier.pdf

Publisher context: U.S. Department of Energy workshop presentation by Midrex
Technologies.

Collected: 2026-07-25

## Verified statements

- The presentation describes a MIDREX H2 case with roughly 90% hydrogen in
  bustle gas and a balance of CO, CO2, H2O and CH4 to obtain 1.4% carbon in DRI.
- It estimates hydrogen consumption at about 650 Nm3/t DRI, equivalent to
  54 kg/t DRI.
- For a 1.6 Mt/y, 200 t/h plant it estimates about 130,000 Nm3/h hydrogen and
  around 650 MW of hydrogen-production capacity under the stated conversion.
- It says up to 30% of natural gas can be substituted by hydrogen without a
  process change, with preheating potentially required as the hydrogen share
  increases.
- It explicitly identifies hydrogen availability, scale-up and competitive
  hydrogen cost as deployment constraints.

The figures are 2018 engineering estimates rather than measured results from a
current pure-hydrogen commercial plant.
