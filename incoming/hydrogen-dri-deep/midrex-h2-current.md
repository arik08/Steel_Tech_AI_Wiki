# MIDREX H2 current process page — evidence excerpt

Source: https://www.midrex.com/midrex-process/midrex-h2/

Collected: 2026-07-25

## Verified statements

- MIDREX designs greenfield plants for up to 100% hydrogen operation or
  majority-hydrogen operation with minimal natural-gas use.
- The design retains the commercial MIDREX shaft furnace and process-gas
  systems. Related large-scale plants already operate at 2.5 Mt/y and with
  reducing gas containing about 55–75% hydrogen.
- The 100% hydrogen configuration adds electric heating for the recirculating
  hydrogen gas.
- The route can make hot DRI for direct charging and HBI for shipment.
- The page describes Stegra's Boden project as a commercial 100% hydrogen DRI
  plant and states a claimed CO2 reduction of up to 95% versus traditional
  steelmaking.

The capacity and emissions statements are supplier descriptions and are not
independent operating results.
