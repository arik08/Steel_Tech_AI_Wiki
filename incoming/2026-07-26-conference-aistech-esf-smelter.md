# Smelter — Green Steelmaking Using Low-Grade DRI

Verified source: Association for Iron & Steel Technology, 2024 AISTech
Conference Proceedings.

Official record: https://imis.aist.org/store/detail.aspx?id=PR-388-199
Product code: PR-388-199.
Published/presented: 2024-05-08.

Authors: Gerald Wimmer, Bernhard Voraberger, Johannes Rosner,
Andreas Pfeiffer.

The official abstract distinguishes furnace selection by DRI feed quality.
It states that an EAF is appropriate for melting and refining high-grade DRI
with manageable slag volume, whereas lower-grade DRI and beneficiation limits
create a need for a separate smelting furnace. The paper compares the
application areas of the smelter and EAF.
