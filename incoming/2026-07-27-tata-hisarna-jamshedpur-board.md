# Tata Steel Board — HIsarna Jamshedpur demonstration

On 10 December 2025 Tata Steel stated that its Board had reviewed HIsarna
pilot-trial progress and scale-up opportunities. The Board approved starting
engineering work and the regulatory-approval process for a demonstration plant
of approximately 1 MTPA capacity in Jamshedpur.

The release says Tata Steel had operated the IJmuiden HIsarna pilot plant for a
decade and had jointly run trials with another global steel company in recent
years. It characterises HIsarna as a low-carbon route able to use lower-quality
iron ore, eliminate coke use and accept steel slag. It also states that Tata
Steel owns the global intellectual-property rights.

The decision is an engineering and regulatory gate. The release does not say
that the Jamshedpur plant has received all permits, reached FID, begun
construction, been commissioned, or produced hot metal.
