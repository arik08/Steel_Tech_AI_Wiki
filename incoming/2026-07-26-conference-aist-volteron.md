# Volteron — Scalable Electrochemical Ironmaking for Green Steel Production

Official presentation material published by AIST for the conference
“Scrap Supplements and Alternative Ironmaking 10,” held in Orlando, Florida,
9–11 March 2026.

The John Cockerill presentation describes Volteron as an alkaline,
low-temperature iron-ore electrolysis route. It states a specific-energy target
below 3.75 MWh per tonne of iron and presents a scale-up roadmap. The roadmap
shows 2026 as the planned signature point for a 40–80 kt/y step-up plant, but
explicitly conditions that schedule on decarbonization regulation through 2030
and on the maturity achieved by the 1 m² pilot.

This is an official conference presentation by the technology developer. It is
not a peer-reviewed journal paper, and its performance targets and roadmap
should be treated as developer claims rather than verified commercial results.
