# Nucor Convent DRI carbon capture and storage agreement

Nucor announced on 2023-06-01 that it had signed an agreement with ExxonMobil
to capture, transport and permanently store carbon dioxide from Nucor's direct
reduced iron plant in Convent, Louisiana.

The announced design capacity is up to 800,000 metric tonnes of carbon dioxide
per year, with storage at an ExxonMobil-owned facility in Louisiana.

The release confirms the contract and design scope from Nucor's side. It does
not confirm commissioning, annual captured volume, capture rate, transport
start or verified permanent injection.
