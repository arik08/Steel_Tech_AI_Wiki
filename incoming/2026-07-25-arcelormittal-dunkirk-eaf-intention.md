# ArcelorMittal Dunkirk EAF investment intention in 2025

ArcelorMittal announced on 2025-05-15 that it intended to invest approximately
EUR 1.2 billion in a first electric arc furnace at Dunkirk.

The company stated that European market conditions and policy implementation
would determine the relaunch of the project. This announcement recorded an
investment intention following delays to European decarbonisation projects; it
preceded the February 2026 confirmation of construction at a revised EUR 1.3
billion.
