# ARENA funding for the NeoSmelt FEED study

On 17 June 2025, the Australian Renewable Energy Agency allocated A$19.8
million to the NeoSmelt project. The funding supports a front-end engineering
design study for the proposed DRI–ESF pilot at Kwinana, Western Australia.

ARENA identified BlueScope, BHP, Rio Tinto, Woodside and Mitsui Iron Ore
Development as the joint-venture participants. The FEED study was intended to
inform a later final investment decision; the funding announcement did not
authorise construction or confirm operation.

The project objective is to demonstrate lower-emissions molten iron production
from Pilbara ore and to generate information needed to assess a future
Australian lower-emissions iron industry.
