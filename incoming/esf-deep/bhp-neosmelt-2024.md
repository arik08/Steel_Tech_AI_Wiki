# NeoSmelt selects Kwinana for the proposed ESF pilot

On 17 December 2024, BHP, BlueScope and Rio Tinto announced Kwinana Industrial
Area in Western Australia as the selected site for the proposed NeoSmelt pilot.
They stated that the collaboration had been formed in February 2024.

The proposed plant is intended to test a DRI–ESF route using Pilbara iron ore
and to produce 30,000–40,000 tonnes of molten iron per year. The direct
reduction plant would initially use natural gas, with a later objective to use
lower-emissions hydrogen. The product would be hot metal suitable for
downstream BOF steelmaking.

The Western Australian Government announced a contribution of A$75 million.
At the date of the release, entry into feasibility was still subject to funding,
the final investment decision was targeted for 2026 and operations were
expected in 2028. These were targets, not completed milestones.

BlueScope was project manager and contributed operating experience from New
Zealand electric smelting. Woodside Energy was announced as a prospective
equal-equity participant and energy supplier, subject to commercial
arrangements.

The release estimated up to 80% lower CO2 emissions intensity than the
conventional BF–BOF route only under renewable electricity and zero-emissions
hydrogen assumptions. It explicitly retained process carbon for producing hot
metal suitable for the BOF.
