# BHP current NeoSmelt and DRI–ESF programme status

BHP reports that NeoSmelt has progressed from pre-feasibility into a final
design phase. Kwinana remains the selected location and the partners are
BlueScope, BHP, Mitsui Iron Ore Development, Rio Tinto and Woodside Energy.

Subject to approvals, BHP states that the project remains on track for
commissioning in the second half of calendar 2028, when it would begin
demonstrating technical viability. This is a current target and not evidence of
construction completion or operation.

BHP also reports FY2025 commercial pellet and DRI trials in China using
Pilbara ores. Those upstream trials support the DRI feed route but did not
constitute operation of the proposed NeoSmelt ESF.
