# METI Green Steel for GX: Kurashiki EAF

Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry described the Kurashiki
conversion in its 2025 Green Steel for GX material.

The government material states that a large electric arc furnace with annual
production capacity of approximately 2 million tonnes is scheduled to be
installed at JFE Steel's West Japan Works Kurashiki district and to begin
commercial production in fiscal year 2028.

This source independently confirms the supported project, location, capacity
and target operating period. It does not confirm construction completion or
actual production.
