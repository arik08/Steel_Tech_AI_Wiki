# ARENA — Calix ZESTY Green Iron Demonstration Plant

ARENA lists Calix Ltd as lead organisation for a ZESTY hydrogen-direct-reduced-
iron / hot-briquetted-iron demonstration project. The recorded project period
is 1 July 2025 through 31 March 2031. The page was last updated 30 March 2026.

ARENA describes ZESTY as an indirectly heated hydrogen DRI route intended to
accept iron-ore fines and lower-grade ores without pelletisation. It states a
theoretical minimum hydrogen use of 54 kg per tonne of iron; this is a
stoichiometric/design proposition, not a demonstrated plant consumption.

Following pilot-scale trials, the project aims to construct, commission and
operate a 30,000 tonne-per-year HDRI demonstration plant and deliver Pre-FEED /
FEED for a commercial-scale plant. The demonstration plant is intended for
toll processing of multiple ore types and grades and for product testing by
multiple steelmakers. The page does not report that the demonstration plant has
already been built, commissioned, or achieved its design rate.
