# Nippon Steel Hasaki hydrogen direct-reduction test furnace

Nippon Steel's March 2026 GX briefing describes a larger-scale test shaft
furnace at the Hasaki R&D Center for hydrogen direct reduction of iron.

The disclosed design basis is approximately 1 tonne per hour, an approximately
80 metre by 200 metre site, and a furnace height of approximately 60 metres.
The process is intended to evaluate reduction, cooling, and molding in a flow,
equipment configuration, and system arrangement similar to a commercial
plant. The development aims to use 100 percent hydrogen and address lower-grade
iron ore.

The roadmap stated FY2025 initiation of test-furnace verification, study of
larger-scale validation from FY2027, and establishment of practical
implementation technology around 2040. Nippon Steel subsequently reported
that the test reduction furnace began operation in March 2026. These milestones
do not establish commercial-scale production.
