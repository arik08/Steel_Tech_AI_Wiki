# ADNOC — Al Reyadah steel-sector CCUS

Official release:
https://www.adnoc.ae/en/news-and-media/press-releases/2017/adnoc-and-masdars-carbon-capture-facility-holds-key-to-limiting-industrial-co2-emissions

Published: 2017-01-19.

ADNOC describes Al Reyadah as the first commercial-scale CCUS facility for the
steel industry. It can process up to 800,000 tonnes of CO2 per year captured
from Emirates Steel. The CO2 is injected into mature Abu Dhabi oil fields for
enhanced oil recovery and reported to remain trapped underground.

Boundary note: Emirates Steel uses gas-based direct reduction rather than a
blast furnace. The project is relevant as an operating steel-sector capture,
compression, pipeline and injection chain, but it is not evidence that a
commercial BF top-gas CCS chain has been demonstrated.
