# Tata Steel — Jamshedpur BF-gas carbon capture plant

Official release:
https://www.tatasteel.com/media/newsroom/press-releases/india/2021/tata-steel-commissions-india-s-first-plant-for-co2-capture-from-blast-furnace-gas-at-jamshedpur/

Published: 2021-09-14.

Tata Steel commissioned a 5 tonnes-per-day CO2 capture plant at Jamshedpur
Works. The plant extracts CO2 directly from uncombusted blast-furnace gas using
amine technology supplied with Carbon Clean. The CO2-depleted gas is returned
to the works gas network at a higher calorific value, while captured CO2 is
made available for on-site reuse.

Tata described the unit as a source of operational data for future scaled
capture facilities and did not claim geological storage or net-zero BF
operation.
