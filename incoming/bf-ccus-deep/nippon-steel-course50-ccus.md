# Nippon Steel — COURSE50, ESCAP and CCUS development

Official page:
https://www.nipponsteel.com/en/sustainability/env/climate/future.html

Collected: 2026-07-25.

Nippon Steel describes COURSE50 as a 2008–2022 development programme. A 12 m3
experimental BF at Kimitsu verified more than 10% BF CO2 reduction using
hydrogen-rich coke-oven gas, while an energy-saving chemical-absorption CO2
separation and capture process was developed.

Super COURSE50 testing began in May 2022. Operational tests in November–
December 2024 achieved a stated 43% reduction in BF CO2 emissions with heated
external hydrogen. The programme target is 50% or more; this is a small
experimental furnace result, not a full-size BF result.

The ESCAP chemical-absorption process is reported to reduce heat consumption by
more than 40% versus general-purpose technology and recover greater than 99.9%
high-purity CO2 from impurity-rich feed gas. Two industrial units are reported
in operation in Japan. Nippon Steel also reports engineering work for three CCS
value chains covering capture, liquefaction, shipping and storage-potential
assessment.
