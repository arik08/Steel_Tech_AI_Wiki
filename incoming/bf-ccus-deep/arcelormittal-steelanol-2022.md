# ArcelorMittal — Steelanol Ghent inauguration

Official release:
https://corporate.arcelormittal.com/media/news/regulatory-news/arcelormittal-inaugurates-flagship-carbon-capture-and-utilisation-project-at-its-steel-plant-in-ghent-belgium

Published: 2022-12-08.

ArcelorMittal inaugurated the EUR 200 million Steelanol CCU plant in Ghent.
LanzaTech biocatalysts convert carbon-rich steelmaking waste gases and waste
biomass gases to ethanol. Nameplate output is 80 million litres of advanced
ethanol per year, and ArcelorMittal stated an expected 125,000 tonnes per year
reduction in Ghent emissions at full production.

The carbon is embodied in a product rather than permanently stored. Climate
performance therefore depends on feed-gas attribution, displaced product,
energy inputs, product use and end-of-life accounting.
