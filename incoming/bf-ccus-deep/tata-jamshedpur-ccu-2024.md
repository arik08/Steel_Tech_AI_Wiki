# Tata Steel FY2023-24 — Jamshedpur CCU operating update

Official annual-report page:
https://www.tatasteel.com/investors/integrated-report-2023-24/shaping-a-cleaner-tomorrow.html

Published in the FY2023-24 integrated-report cycle.

Tata Steel reports that the 5 TPD amine capture plant had run continuously
24/7 for more than one year. Captured CO2 purity is reported as 97% on a wet
basis. Uses reported are wastewater pH reduction and LD-converter bottom
injection replacing nitrogen or argon. Tata states that more units are planned.

This is utilization and process substitution at small scale; the page does not
quantify durable storage or a plant-wide net avoided-emissions balance.
