# European Commission CORDIS — ULCOS New Blast Furnace Process

Official project page:
https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/RFSR-CT-2004-00005

Project start: 2004-07-01.
Project end: 2009-06-30.
Status on CORDIS: closed.
Coordinator: thyssenkrupp Steel AG.
Total cost: EUR 8,551,812.
EU contribution: EUR 5,131,088.

The project developed a low-CO2 blast-furnace route through modeling,
laboratory work and technical-scale testing. The intended outcome was
experimentally supported process data and preparatory engineering for a future
large pilot. In the wider ULCOS programme, top-gas recycling with CO2 capture
and storage was selected as one of four priority breakthrough routes.

The CORDIS record does not establish operation of a full-size commercial
TGR-BF with geological storage.
