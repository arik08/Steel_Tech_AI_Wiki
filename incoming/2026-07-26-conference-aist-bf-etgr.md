# Profitable Decarbonization of the Blast Furnace Through Electrified Top-Gas Recycling

Official presentation material published by AIST for the conference
“Scrap Supplements and Alternative Ironmaking 10,” held in Orlando, Florida,
9–11 March 2026.

Evan Haas of Helix Carbon presents electrified top-gas recycling as a
blast-furnace retrofit concept. The presentation combines top-gas recycling,
electrification, and carbon-management claims in a proposed route for reducing
blast-furnace emissions.

This is a proprietary technology-provider presentation, not a peer-reviewed
paper or independent plant validation. The document is useful for identifying
the proposed configuration and the developer’s current conference positioning,
but quantitative performance claims require corroboration from operating data
or independent studies.
