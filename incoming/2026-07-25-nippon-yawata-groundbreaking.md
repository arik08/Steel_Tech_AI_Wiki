# Nippon Steel starts Yawata large-EAF conversion construction

On 2026-04-15 Nippon Steel held a groundbreaking ceremony and started the
conversion construction at the Yawata Area of Kyushu Works. The company
describes the project as its first attempt to establish an integrated
high-grade-steel mass-production system using a large electric arc furnace.

The Yawata construction is one part of the broader three-site program covering
new installation at Yawata, expansion at Hirohata, and modification and
restart at Shunan. Groundbreaking at Yawata is not evidence that the other two
sites had started construction on the same date.
