# Chemical and electrochemical pathways to low-carbon iron and steel

Verified source: npj Materials Sustainability, volume 2, article 33, 2024.
Published 2024-10-01.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s44296-024-00036-6
Official article: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44296-024-00036-6

Authors: Kerry Rippy, Robert T. Bell, Noemi Leick.

This open-access review compares current blast-furnace, electric-arc-furnace,
and smelting practice with lower-carbon routes. The reviewed alternatives
include hydrogen direct reduction, hydrogen plasma smelting, hydrogen
smelting, ammonia reduction, aqueous and molten-oxide electrolysis, and
biocarbon use in EAFs.

The authors identify cross-route constraints rather than a single universal
winner. These include low-carbon electricity supply, hydrogen production,
ore-grade limits, scrap copper accumulation, process heat, and scale-up of
electrochemical cells.

Evidence boundary: route descriptions and comparisons are literature-review
findings. They are not proof that every named route has reached commercial
operation.
