# Nippon Steel FY2025 results: carbon-neutral technology progress

Nippon Steel reported on 2026-06-02 that it had decided in May 2025 to invest
in the new installation, expansion, or restart of three electric arc furnaces
at Kyushu Works Yawata, Setouchi Works Hirohata, and Yamaguchi Works Shunan by
fiscal 2029. The investments were selected for government support under
Japan's GX Promotion Act.

For Super COURSE50, the company reported that its small test blast furnace at
the Kimitsu Area achieved a 45 percent reduction in CO2 emissions between
February and March 2026, renewing its previously reported best result.

At the Hasaki R&D Center, Nippon Steel constructed a test reduction furnace for
producing reduced iron from low-grade iron ore using hydrogen and began
operation in March 2026. The company characterizes these activities as
development of high-grade production in large EAFs, 100 percent hydrogen use
in direct reduction, and hydrogen injection into blast furnaces; the reported
results are development milestones, not evidence of commercial-scale hydrogen
steel production.
