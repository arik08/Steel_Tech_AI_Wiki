# ΣIDERWIN—A New Route for Iron Production

Verified sources:

- Official DOI: https://doi.org/10.3390/materproc2021005058
- EU CORDIS publication record:
  https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/768788/results

Authors: Sevasti Koutsoupa, Stavroula Koutalidi, Efthymios Balomenos,
Dimitrios Panias.

This conference paper was published in Materials Proceedings, volume 5,
article 58, for the RawMat 2021 International Conference on Raw Materials and
Circular Economy.

The paper presents the ΣIDERWIN low-temperature alkaline iron-electrowinning
route and discusses bauxite residue as an iron-bearing feed candidate.
The EU CORDIS record classifies it as a conference proceeding and separately
lists peer-reviewed papers, theses, and other SIDERWIN conference outputs.

Evidence boundary: the conference paper supports the process route and
research program. It does not by itself demonstrate commercial production.
