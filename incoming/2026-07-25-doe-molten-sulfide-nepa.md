# DOE NEPA review: Iron Production by Molten Sulfide Electrolysis

The U.S. Department of Energy published categorical exclusion CX-032248 on
2024-10-15 for proposed federal funding to the Massachusetts Institute of
Technology.

The project covers the design, development, fabrication and field testing of
sulfidation and electrolysis equipment for iron production. Work is planned at
MIT Building 5 in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The associated project description
identifies handling and control requirements for gases associated with the
process, including carbon monoxide, carbon dioxide, sulfur dioxide, sulfur and
hydrogen sulfide. These are project design and environmental-review statements,
not evidence that an integrated pilot has completed operation.
