# Evolving Scrapyard: Integrating New Solutions for Advanced Scrap Management

Official presentation material published by AIST for the conference
“Scrap Supplements and Alternative Ironmaking 10,” held in Orlando, Florida,
9–11 March 2026.

Marcelo Saparrat of Tecnoap presents an integrated digital scrap-intake
workflow. The stated scope includes automated scrap classification, truck-box
volumetry and weight capture, density determination, scrap-chemistry
integration, MES/ERP integration, hazardous-object detection, and scrap
characterization. The presentation reports 96% accuracy for truck-container
detection and 76% recall for hazardous-object detection in the illustrated
models.

These are conference-presentation results from the solution supplier, not a
peer-reviewed validation or a guarantee of performance at other sites. The
reported metrics therefore remain tied to the presented test configuration.
