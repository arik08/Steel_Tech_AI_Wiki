# G7 roadmap for standards-based critical-mineral markets

On 31 October 2025, the G7 published a roadmap to promote standards-based markets for
critical minerals. It states that participating governments may use procurement, tax codes,
financial incentives, investment policy, trade measures, price floors, and similar mechanisms
to reduce dependence on supply chains dominated by non-market policies and practices. The
roadmap also calls for greater supply-chain traceability and transparent market practices.

This source note preserves facts stated on the official G7 Canada page. Whether a particular
POSCO project would qualify for any support mechanism is not established by the source.
