# Australia Critical Minerals Production Tax Incentive

The Australian Government states that the Critical Minerals Production Tax Incentive will be
available from 1 July 2027 to 30 June 2040. It provides a refundable, uncapped tax offset equal
to 10% of eligible Australian processing and refining expenditure for up to ten years per
registered activity. Eligible activities must substantially transform a feedstock into a purer or
chemically distinct critical-mineral form. Mining, beneficiation, manufacturing, feedstock cost,
financing cost, and capital expenditure are excluded. Eligible feedstock may include imported
ore, concentrates, tailings, waste streams, and lithium-battery black mass.

This source note preserves facts stated on the official Department of Industry, Science and
Resources page. Project-specific eligibility and cash value require separate tax and cost review.
