# POSCO International 2024 audited disclosure on Myanmar gas-field uncertainty

- Publisher: POSCO INTERNATIONAL
- Reporting date: 2024-12-31
- Original: https://www.poscointl.com/upload/file/202506/202506jSC_mILrzPv.pdf
- Collected: 2026-08-20

## Confirmed company disclosure

The audited financial-statement notes identify the Myanmar A-1/A-3 blocks as gas development and production joint arrangements in which POSCO International holds a 51 percent interest. The same notes state that MOGE holds a 15 percent interest in the project.

The company disclosed that EU and U.S. economic and financial sanctions had been announced against MOGE. As of 31 December 2024, the gas-field business was operating normally, while the company continued to monitor possible additional international sanctions. The company said the future effect on the Myanmar gas-field business could not be reasonably estimated and therefore was not reflected in the financial statements.

## Interpretation boundary

This disclosure is evidence of the company's ownership, normal operating status at the reporting date and management's uncertainty assessment. It does not prove that later sanctions changes have no effect, and it does not disclose transaction-level payment, banking, insurance or foreign-exchange routes.
