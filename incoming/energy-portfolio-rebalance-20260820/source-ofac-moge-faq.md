# OFAC frequently asked questions on the MOGE financial-services directive

- Publisher: U.S. Department of the Treasury, Office of Foreign Assets Control
- Released: 2023-10-31
- Original: https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/added/2023-10-31
- Collected: 2026-08-20

## Confirmed official facts

OFAC FAQ 1138 explains that the MOGE directive covers financial services such as loans, transfers, accounts, insurance, investments, securities, guarantees, foreign exchange, letters of credit and commodity futures or options.

FAQ 1139 explains that OFAC's 50 Percent Rule applies to MOGE property interests. The restriction therefore extends to an entity, including a subsidiary or joint venture, that is owned 50 percent or more by MOGE, directly or indirectly, unless an exception or authorisation applies.

## Interpretation boundary

The 50 percent threshold does not mean every joint venture in which MOGE has any interest is automatically treated as MOGE. Transaction-specific review is still required because services provided directly or indirectly for MOGE's benefit can be restricted even when a joint venture is below the ownership threshold.
