# POSCO International official energy portfolio overview

- Publisher: POSCO INTERNATIONAL
- Original: https://www.poscointl.com/eng/energy
- Collected: 2026-08-20

## Confirmed company exposure

POSCO International describes its energy business as an integrated liquefied-natural-gas value chain. The chain spans gas-field development and production, trading, transportation, LNG-terminal storage and LNG combined-cycle power generation. The company says this structure was completed through its merger with POSCO ENERGY.

The portfolio therefore has more than one decision route. Upstream production affects owned volume and cash flow; trading and transport affect destination and freight options; terminal storage affects timing and throughput; power generation converts fuel cost and system rules into generation margin.

## Interpretation boundary

The page confirms the business structure, not the economics of a specific cargo or contract. Contract prices, destination rights, terminal slots, inventory, power-plant dispatch and hedging positions require internal data.
