# OFAC Directive 1 on financial services involving MOGE

- Publisher: U.S. Department of the Treasury, Office of Foreign Assets Control
- Dated: 2023-10-31
- Effective: 2023-12-15
- Original: https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/932251/download?inline
- Collected: 2026-08-20

## Confirmed official facts

Directive 1 under Executive Order 14014 identifies Myanma Oil and Gas Enterprise (MOGE) as an agency or instrumentality of the Government of Burma. It prohibits U.S. persons, from 15 December 2023, from providing, exporting or re-exporting financial services directly or indirectly to or for the benefit of MOGE or its property or interests in property, unless otherwise authorised.

The directive also states that other activities with MOGE or involving MOGE property or interests remain permitted when they are not prohibited by another law, the executive order or another OFAC sanctions programme. Transactions intended to evade or avoid the directive are prohibited.

## Interpretation boundary

The directive is a financial-services restriction rather than a blanket prohibition on all gas-field operations. Whether a payment, insurance, foreign-exchange, guarantee or other service is affected depends on the parties, ownership and transaction route. The source does not quantify POSCO International's exposure.
