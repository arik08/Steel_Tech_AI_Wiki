# U.S. Energy Department approves additional Plaquemines LNG exports

- Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy
- Published: 2026-03-13
- Original: https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-approves-immediate-additional-lng-exports-plaquemines-lng
- Collected: 2026-08-20

## Confirmed official facts

The U.S. Secretary of Energy authorised an immediate 13 percent increase in exports from Venture Global's Plaquemines liquefied-natural-gas terminal in Louisiana. The authorisation permitted up to an additional 0.45 billion cubic feet per day of exports to non-free-trade-agreement countries.

The Department said the terminal was thereby authorised to export a total of 3.85 billion cubic feet per day to free-trade-agreement and non-free-trade-agreement destinations.

## Interpretation boundary

The order raises authorised export volume; it does not guarantee that the full volume will be produced, loaded or delivered to Asia. Actual market supply depends on plant operation, feedgas, shipping, contracts and destination economics.
