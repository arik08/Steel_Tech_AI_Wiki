# DOE Round 5 selection: UK IDEA dual-loop capture at Nucor Steel Gallatin

The U.S. Department of Energy selected a University of Kentucky Research
Foundation project to add a polishing carbon-capture loop with hydrogen
production to an existing aqueous-amine-solvent capture unit at Nucor Steel
Gallatin.

The engineering-scale scope includes design and construction of the polishing
loop, integration with the existing capture unit, testing and analysis of the
combined system, and measurement of solvent degradation products and criteria
pollutants. The stated target is flue gas with less than 100 ppm CO2 after the
dual-loop treatment.

DOE funding is USD 3,000,000, non-DOE funding is USD 750,001, and total project
value is USD 3,750,001. The selection describes planned development and
demonstration work, not a completed commercial carbon-capture installation.
