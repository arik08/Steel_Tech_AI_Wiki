# Nippon Steel three-site EAF conversion investment decision

Nippon Steel's 2025 investment decision covers construction of one new EAF at
Kyushu Works Yawata, expansion of one EAF at Setouchi Works Hirohata, and
modification and restart of one EAF at Yamaguchi Works Shunan. The scope also
includes equipment for high-grade steel manufacturing, logistics, power
supply, and downstream-process energy measures.

The disclosed amounts and capacities are:

- Yawata: JPY 630.2 billion investment, maximum JPY 179.9 billion government
  support, approximately 2.0 million tonnes per year, production planned for
  the second half of fiscal 2029.
- Hirohata: JPY 140.0 billion investment, maximum JPY 42.8 billion government
  support, approximately 0.5 million tonnes per year, production planned for
  the second half of fiscal 2029.
- Shunan: JPY 98.5 billion investment, maximum JPY 28.7 billion government
  support, approximately 0.4 million tonnes per year, production planned for
  the second half of fiscal 2028.
- Total: JPY 868.7 billion investment, maximum JPY 251.4 billion government
  support, and approximately 2.9 million tonnes per year.

The company presents the program as conversion from blast-furnace processes to
EAF processes at high-grade steel manufacturing bases. The investment
decision does not by itself establish completed construction or stable
commercial production.
