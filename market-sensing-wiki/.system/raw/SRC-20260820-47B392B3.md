# IEA Gas Market Report Q3 2026 executive summary

- Publisher: International Energy Agency
- Published: 2026-07-07
- Original: https://www.iea.org/reports/gas-market-report-q3-2026/executive-summary
- Collected: 2026-08-20

## Confirmed market observations and forecast

The IEA reported that LNG loadings from Qatar and the United Arab Emirates fell by 35 billion cubic metres year-on-year between March and June 2026. Non-Gulf LNG production rose by almost 18 percent, or about 27 billion cubic metres, over the same period and offset about three quarters of the Gulf decline.

For full-year 2026, the IEA forecast combined Qatar and UAE LNG supply to fall by about 45 percent, or 54 billion cubic metres, from 2025. New projects in North America, Africa and Australia were expected to add close to 50 billion cubic metres, while legacy projects were expected to add more than 10 billion cubic metres. On these assumptions, global LNG trade would remain broadly flat year-on-year.

The forecast assumes that the Strait of Hormuz fully reopens during the third quarter of 2026 and that undamaged regional facilities return by early in the fourth quarter. Delays could push the market into its first annual decline in LNG supply since 2012.

## Interpretation boundary

The regional supply figures are an IEA forecast and scenario, not confirmed POSCO International volumes or prices. They establish the scale and location of the supply shift. Company impact requires contract-level destination rights, freight, terminal slots, inventory and power-fuel requirements.
