# EU Raw Materials Mechanism first buyer-supplier call

On 13 April 2026, the European Commission launched the first call under the Raw Materials
Mechanism. The platform allows buyers of critical raw materials to aggregate demand and
connect with suppliers, financial institutions, and storage providers. It focuses on strategic
sectors including rare earths, defence, and battery materials. The Commission describes the
tool as voluntary and market-based and says it complements existing commercial relationships.

This source note preserves facts stated on the official European Commission page. It does not
assume that participation guarantees an offtake contract or project financing.
